# Copy library

Words decide quality more than motion does. Use these patterns, then fill them with the
user's real facts. Every number, price, date, result and review in an example below is
illustrative: never ship it unless the user confirmed it.

## 25 hook formulas

Beat 1 is on screen at frame 0 (it is the thumbnail), lands its payoff inside 3 s and uses
under 10 words on screen. Pick the formula that fits the facts you have.

| # | Formula | Scene | English | Hinglish |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Price shock | `hook` + `strike` | Agency quote: ~~₹50,000~~. Now? *One prompt.* | Agency ka quote: ~~₹50,000~~. Ab? *Ek prompt.* |
| 2 | Time shock | `hook` + `strike` | It took ~~2 hours~~. Now *10 seconds.* | ~~2 ghante~~ ka kaam. Ab *10 second.* |
| 3 | The question they already ask | `kinetic` | Why is your reel stuck at *200 views?* | Reel pe *200 views* hi kyun aate hain? |
| 4 | Common mistake | `title` | You're editing reels *the hard way.* | Aap reels *galat tarike* se bana rahe ho. |

Hook rules: one accent (the payoff word), no logo intro, no "Hi guys", no slow fade-in. If the
user is unsure, offer three angles: a question (3), a demonstration (7), a mistake (4).

## 15 CTA patterns

One action per video. The button (`action`) is 1-3 words; everything else goes in `kicker` / `sub`.

| # | Pattern | `action` | `kicker` / `sub` (EN · Hinglish) | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Comment keyword | `VIDEO` | Comment karo 👇 · "Prompt DM mein bhejunga" | The creator will really DM it |
| 2 | Part 2 | `Follow` | Part 2 tomorrow · "Part 2 ke liye follow karo" | A series exists |
| 3 | Save | `Save this` | For your next shoot · "Baad mein kaam aayega" | Tips, checklists, recipes |
| 4 | Share | `Send this` | To a friend who needs it · "Us dost ko bhejo" | Relatable / useful content |
| 5 | Link in bio | `Shop now` | Link in bio · "Link bio mein hai" | Instagram / TikTok sales |
| 6 | Quiet link | `Try Lumen` (link style) | The promise as the headline | Launch films, premium brands |
| 7 | Book a call | `Book a call` | Free 15-min call · "Free call book karo" | Services, coaches, agencies |
| 8 | WhatsApp | `WhatsApp us` | The number in `sub` · "Abhi WhatsApp karo" | Local business, India |
| 9 | Visit | `Visit us` | Address + hours · "MG Road wale store aao" | Shops, cafés, clinics |
| 10 | Code | `Shop now` | Use *GLOW20* for 20% off · "Code GLOW20 lagao" | D2C offers |
| 11 | Deadline | `Order now` | Ends Sunday · "Sirf Sunday tak" | Sales, launches |
| 12 | Download | `Download now` | Free on iOS & Android | Apps |
| 13 | Book a seat | `Book now` | Seats are limited · "Seat book karo" | Events, workshops (only say "limited" if true) |
| 14 | Apply | `Apply now` | Know someone? Tag them · "Dost ko tag karo" | Hiring |
| 15 | Poll | `Comment 1 or 2` | Which would you pick? · "Aap kaunsa loge?" | Engagement, A/B products |

The `say` of the CTA beat reads the kicker first, then the action ("Know someone? Tag them. Or
apply now."): on-screen elements appear in the order they are spoken.

## Transition words

They open the next beat's `say` and glue beats into one story. Usually spoken only; put one on
screen only when it is the punch ("Ab? *Ek prompt.*"). At most one per beat.

- **English**: But here's the thing · So · Now · Here's how · The result? · And the best part? ·
  Plus · Still not sold? · Which means · That's why · Finally · One more thing
- **Hinglish**: Lekin · Toh · Ab · Aur sabse badi baat? · Result? · Matlab · Isliye · Aur haan ·
  Bas · Ek aur cheez · Socho · Dekho

## Say vs show

`say` is what the voice speaks; the on-screen fields are the "show". Words appear as they are
spoken, so the two must line up.

1. **Same words, same order.** Show may drop words but never reorder them or add words that
   aren't spoken (unspoken text falls back to estimated timing).
2. **Kickers are spoken first or not at all.** If the kicker is said at the end of the beat,
   everything after it waits.
3. **Say full sentences, show the headline.**

   | say | show |
   |---|---|
   | "Agency ka quote? Fifty thousand. Ab? Bas ek prompt." | setup `Agency ka quote:` · strike `₹50,000` · punch `Ab? *Ek prompt.*` |
   | "Seventy-five milliseconds from text to sound." | value `75ms` · label `from text to *sound*` |
   | "Why switch? Agencies take two weeks. We take ten minutes." | compare `2 weeks` vs `10 minutes` |

4. **Demo beats describe, they don't recite.** For `prompt` / `chat`, say what is happening
   ("Type what you need and it answers in seconds"); the typed text doesn't need to be spoken.
5. **Length.** Speech runs ~2.6 words/s. A 3-6 s beat needs ~8-15 spoken words; a `prompt`
   demo needs 12+; the hook reaches its payoff within ~7 spoken words.
6. **Every beat is spoken** in a narrated video, except a silent `logo` outro.

## Numbers rule

Digits on screen, words in `say` (the checker warns about digits in `say`). Match how the
speaker talks: Hinglish creators often say numbers in English.

| Screen | say (English) | say (Hinglish / Hindi) |
|---|---|---|
| `₹999` | nine ninety-nine | nau sau ninyanave |
| `₹1,50,000` | one lakh fifty thousand rupees | dedh lakh rupaye |
| `₹1.2 Cr` | one point two crore | ek point do crore |
| `30%` | thirty percent | tees percent |
| `4.8★` | four point eight stars | four point eight stars |
| `3x` | three times | teen guna |
| `10M+` | over ten million | ek crore se zyada |
| `75ms` | seventy-five milliseconds | seventy-five milliseconds |
| `2026` | twenty twenty-six | do hazaar chhabbees |
| `12 Oct` | twelfth October | baarah October |
| `7 pm` | seven pm | shaam saat baje |
| `98765 43210` | nine eight seven six five, four three two one zero | same, in groups |

- Use the Indian system (lakh, crore, `1,50,000`) for Indian audiences, the western one otherwise.
- Round for the screen (`2.4M`), never round a claim up, and spell a number identically on every beat.
- `bars` values are plain numbers; the unit goes in `unit`.

## Length by platform

Recommendations, not platform limits. The checker warns past 60 s (vertical, square,
portrait) and 180 s (landscape).

| Where | Format | Sweet spot | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | `reel` | 15-30 s | Bottom ~30 % is covered by captions; the safe zone handles it |
| YouTube Shorts | `reel` | 20-45 s | Shorts loop: an ending that flows back to the hook replays well |
| TikTok | `reel` | 15-35 s | |
| Stories / WhatsApp Status | `reel` | 15-30 s | One offer per video; the date / number in big type |
| Instagram / LinkedIn feed | `portrait` | 15-45 s | Many feed viewers start muted: the screen text alone must tell the story |
| Feed ads | `square` / `portrait` | 6 / 15 / 30 s | Brand + offer inside the first 3 s |
| YouTube, website hero, keynote | `landscape` | 30-60 s | Launch films 35-50 s, 9 beats |
| X / Twitter | `landscape` or `square` | 15-45 s | |

## Professional look checklist

Run through it before `npm run make`.

- [ ] Frame 0 shows the topic; the hook's payoff lands inside 3 s.
- [ ] One idea per beat, one accent per scene, on the word that matters (number, name, verb).
- [ ] Big type ≤ 7 words; kickers are labels (≤ 26 chars); no paragraphs anywhere.
- [ ] Every name, price, date and number is spelled identically on every beat.
- [ ] Never three scenes of the same type in a row; dense beats (list, grid, compare) alternate with airy ones (title, stat, kinetic).
- [ ] At most two `bg: accent` / `inverse` beats, saved for the most important moments.
- [ ] One theme, one motion personality; brand colours come from `npm run brand`, not guesses.
- [ ] Emoji: at most one per line, in casual themes (midnight, pop, desi). Calm themes (studio, editorial, corporate, mono) stay emoji-free except grid icons.
- [ ] Type case is left to the theme (don't type ALL CAPS yourself); no "!!!"; calm themes end statements with a full stop.
- [ ] `say` sounds natural read aloud and matches the screen word for word, in order.
- [ ] Hindi stays in one script per line (Devanagari or Latin, not mixed).
- [ ] The CTA asks for one thing in 1-3 words; the video ends on `cta` (or `cta` + silent `logo`).
- [ ] No `[CAPS]` placeholders left; every claim confirmed by the user; AI footage tagged `"generated": true`.
- [ ] Music is a licensed track the user supplied; nothing else.
- [ ] `npm run check` and `npm run qa` are both clean.
