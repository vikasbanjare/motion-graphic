# CRED card film v3: storyboard

The v2 storyboard followed the reference's techniques instead of the product's story, and the user rejected it. Their notes:
- the intro made no sense;
- random objects appeared;
- there was no link to the product;
- the frames were cluttered;
- the lens was imprecise;
- several beats had no text;
- the end card looked bad;
- the SFX were too loud.

The one thing they kept was the flat-to-3D switch near the end.

## Rules for v3

1. **The card is the hero of every beat.** Nothing appears on screen unless it explains a benefit of the card.
2. **One idea per beat.** Each beat has one subject and one headline, and the text is on screen for the whole beat. The film must read with the sound off.
3. **One connecting element: reward coins.** Engraved coins are earned by spending, then travel into each benefit. Every transition is the coin, or the card, moving. No cut ever brings in an unexplained object.
4. **Clean frames.** One hero, at most one or two supporting objects, a calm duotone background and generous empty space for the text.
5. **One lens move only.** "Take a closer look" at the card, motivated, with a single smooth push-in and push-out. No lens swaps.
6. **Sound is motivated and quiet.** Every SFX belongs to something visible: a pen scratch, a coin clink, a card swipe, a page turn. Each sits 2–6 dB over the music bed, as measured on CRED's own films, never on top of it.

**Facts on screen:** only the sourced claims are used. Two pieces of copy go beyond them:
- the "₹0" fee tag illustrates "zero joining fee", which is sourced;
- the word "introducing" is a framing word, not a claim.

## End card: what was wrong in v2 and what changes

- **Problems:**
  - the black slab looked flat and cheap;
  - it had no thickness or edge light;
  - the foil stripe was too thick;
  - the details were muddy.
- **Fix:**
  - **Card body:** an engraved metal finish in the film's own line screen, with real thickness and bevel, light rolling across it, and a thin foil stripe.
  - **Chip:** finer detail.
  - **Composition:** a calm floor shadow, and the card placed at the golden section.
