# CRED IndusInd Bank RuPay credit card: 10 concepts and storyboards

Made with `skills/art-director/SKILL.md`, with research in `docs/research/creative-ideation.md`. This is unofficial spec work.

**Facts allowed on screen (only these):**
- the CRED IndusInd Bank RuPay credit card;
- 5% rewards on online shopping;
- zero joining fee;
- redeem on flights, hotels and 2,000+ products on CRED store.

Craft rules come from `docs/research/cred-money/BUILD-SPEC.md` and `docs/research/pro-film-rules.md`.

## 1. Proposition, material and verb

- **Single-minded proposition:** *Everyday online spending quietly turns into trips and things you love, and the card costs nothing to join.*
- **Native material:** the card itself:
  - the gold chip with its contact pads;
  - the embossed numerals;
  - the holographic foil strip;
  - the engraved guilloche face;
  - the metal edge;
  - the receipt it produces.
- **Native verbs:** pay, earn (5%), redeem, travel, unlock.

## 2. Ideation log (32 ideas; each tagged with the method that produced it)

**Creativity templates:**
1. **Chip city** (dimensionality: scale). The camera dives into the chip, whose pads are city districts.
2. **Embossed numerals** (dimensionality). The card's raised digits become architecture: 5 is the reward tower, 0 is the gate, 2,000 is a skyline.
3. **Coin mountain** (extreme situation). 5% piles into a mountain you climb to a plane. *Killed: coins cliché.*
4. **Receipt road** (consequences). The receipt keeps printing and becomes the road to every reward.
5. **The fee that never catches up** (competition). A "joining fee" stamp chases the card and only ever prints 0.
6. **Find the zero** (interactive experiment). An engraved puzzle where the viewer spots the hidden 0.
7. **One card, many doors** (pictorial analogy / replacement). The card is the key to a shop, an airport gate, a hotel room and a store locker.
8. **Card as boarding pass** (replacement). *Merged into 7.*
9. **Card as hotel key** (replacement). *Merged into 7.*
10. **Years in seconds** (dimensionality: time). A year of shopping as a time-lapse of a city lighting up. *Merged into 1.*

**Visual rhetoric (hero fusions and replacements):**
11. **Origami card** (fusion). The engraved card folds into a plane, a house and a box.
12. **Prism** (fusion). Light through the foil strip splits into a spectrum, and each colour band is a world.
13. **Guilloche maze** (replacement). One engraved line of the card's pattern is a road through every world.
14. **Departures board** (replacement). The card's benefits flip into place as destinations on a split-flap board.
15. **Card as passport** (replacement). *Too close to 7.*

**Bisociation and blending (card × distant frame):**
16. **Card × constellations.** Purchases light stars, and rewards join them into star maps; a telescope is the loupe.
17. **Card × tide.** The foil strip is a shoreline. *Weak link to the facts.*
18. **Card × orchestra.** Each benefit is an instrument. *Abstract, hard to read muted.*
19. **Card × bonsai.** Spending grows a tree. *Killed: flowers are too close to the reference.*
20. **Card × typewriter.** Benefits are typed. *Kinetic type only, no world.*
21. **Card × weather.** A forecast of rewards. *Gimmick.*
22. **Card × train timetable.** *Merged into 14.*
23. **Card × mint.** *Done in night 03, weak.*
24. **Card × watermark.** *Done in night 04, weak.*
25. **Card × lighthouse.** *Killed: from the reference.*
26. **Card × museum columns.** *Killed: from the reference.*
27. **Card × pinball.** Purchases ricochet and light up targets. *Fun but off-tone.*
28. **Card × kaleidoscope.** *Merged into 12.*
29. **Card × pop-up book.** Each page pops a benefit world out of the flat card. *Strong; merged into 11 as an option.*
30. **Card × tailor's measuring tape.** *Weak.*
31. **Card × domino run.** One purchase tips a chain to a plane. *Fun; kept as a transition idea.*
32. **Card × sand / time.** *Weak.*

**Kill list applied:** coins raining, bags flying, icons in a row, spinning globe alone, phone UI as hero, and every composition from the reference.

## 4. Shared production rules (all 10)

- **Length:** 20–30 s, 24 fps, 1920×1080, built on a music grid chosen per film.
- **3D:**
  - built from the engraving material (`v2/three.tsx`), with CC0 scans where they fit;
  - one duotone per world;
  - foil only on the hero;
  - lens vignette only when there is a lens.
- **Camera:** one continuous journey per film, or motivated transitions only. Every transition names the shared element it carries (a match cut or a fusion).
- **Copy:** every beat carries its line for the whole beat, so the film reads muted. Only the facts above appear.
- **Ending:** the governing metaphor resolves into the real card, then CRED's own logo file, unmodified.
- **Music:** chosen per concept (see each board). No reuse of the same bed.
- **Gate:**
  - the reference scorecard;
  - a side-by-side frame review against the reference;
  - film_qa;
  - a thumbnail sheet reviewed before the motion render.

**One sentence:** the card's raised numerals rise into a city built from the numbers that matter: 5, 0 and 2,000.

**Music:** a minimal, warm piano-and-marimba pulse at 112 BPM, with a deep sub on each numeral landing.
