## Notes


#### N001 Select Sector SPDRs 2020 [3D-product-CGI][brand-system]
Whole world built from wood and laminate: miniature city of wooden blocks, wooden mannequins, ticker letters (XLF, XLB, XLE, XLU) as giant carved wood type in the landscape. Each sector = one diorama (wood jet engine, helicopter, DNA ladder, wind turbines, factory pipes, red housing blocks) with the sector ticker label. Palette per sector (lime, violet, cyan, yellow, red) on warm wood. Camera glides and tilts between dioramas; logo on a round wooden badge in an overhead circular layout. 35 shots / 31 s (0.89 s). One material (wood) unifies a dozen ideas.

#### N002 Dreams [2D-character][flat-illustration]
Vertical. Single locked composition: red sky, a giant B&W photo of a sleeping face as the horizon; tiny glowing yellow silhouette figures run, flip and dance across the face with green particle trails. 35 s, one shot. Scale contrast (tiny figure vs huge face) and one hue for the character.

#### N003 Mamá no quiero ser artista [2D-character][kinetic-type]
Marquee lightbulb title on a wall, dolly into a dreamy blurred bokeh world; hand-drawn sparkles and a star mascot, polaroid cards fly; a circular porthole showing a road landscape with balloon; cartoon hands; black spiky character with glowing eyes. Small yellow subtitles per line. Mixed media: 2D drawn over blurred photo backgrounds.

#### N004 Discord: Safety Teen Charter [2D-character][brand-system]
Discord blurple world: a cartoon flying ship/train full of characters, portal ring, glossy 3D-shaded 2D characters with thick shapes, speed lines, crowd scenes, #drivetime signs. Final logo floats over clouds. Rich 2D character animation, 8 shots / 30 s, camera tracks constantly.

#### N005 AI animal portraits (Drive link title) [3D-product-CGI][ai-generated]
AI-generated-looking hero shots: iridescent jellyfish rotating against peach/teal gradient, then a series of animal portraits framed in pastel flora on flat colour backgrounds (giraffe on peach, rabbit on pink, wolf on cream, parrot) with slow push-ins and a letterbox-strip transition. Typical of AI-plate films: each shot is one subject on a clean colour field.

#### N006 RSquad: TON Rust node [2D-character][archival-montage][grunge]
Black-and-white ink/brush comic style (AI-assisted look) with heavy contrast and red armbands as the only colour; a courier character, airships over a city bridge, crowd and police. Subtitles bottom-centre. 89 shots in 180 s (2.0 s). Monochrome + one accent colour narrative.

#### N007 TED (story) [2D-character][flat-illustration]
Grey-scale hand-painted textures, a boy character with pink cheeks, family on a hill under a white sunburst, extreme close-up eye with tear, giant feet stepping, a figure tangled in strings, chains, a dark creature. Purple as the only accent (a toy). Tiny subtitles. Emotional editorial explainer.

#### N008 Teka built-in max series [3D-product-CGI]
Photoreal 3D kitchen: warm walnut panels, built-in ovens and fridge; camera dollies along the wall, panels open to reveal interiors, macro on shelves and hinges, sunlight shadows through windows; colourway swap at the end (wood → sage green). Product film = one set, many slow moves, material swap.

#### N009 Saka Sama music video [kinetic-type][flat-illustration]

Japanese title card in red/black heavy type. Live action composited into 2D game worlds.
