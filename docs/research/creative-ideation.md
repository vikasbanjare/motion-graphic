## 2026-10-10: first pass

### 1. Creativity templates (Goldenberg, Mazursky & Solomon, 1999)

- **Paper:** "The Fundamental Templates of Quality Ads", *Marketing Science* 18(3), 333–351 ([PDF via Columbia Business School](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/pubfiles/3384/fundamental.pdf)).
- **Finding** [Certain, per multiple summaries]: six abstract "creativity templates" explained 89% of 200 highly rated, award-contest ads.
- **Comparison:** 50% of award-winning ads followed them, against 2.5% of non-winning ads.
- **Training effect:** people trained on the templates produced ads rated more creative than those from brainstorming-trained groups ([Wylie summary](https://wyliecomm.com/2022/11/creative-templates); [Humber](https://mediaarts.humber.ca/current-students/resources/research/past-projects/can-the-creativity-templates-of-quality-advertising-also-predict-market-effectiveness.html); [human factors summary](https://www.humanfactors.com/Newsletters/six_ways_to_think_out_of_the_box.html); [DIY worksheet](https://oercollective.caul.edu.au/app/uploads/sites/163/2024/12/Goldenbergs-Templates-DIY-worksheet.pdf)).
- **The template names** [Likely, recalled; secondary sources list different labels]:
  - pictorial analogy (replacement);
  - extreme situation;
  - consequences;
  - competition;
  - interactive experiment;
  - dimensionality alteration.

  Verify the names against the paper's PDF when the network allows.
- **Lesson for us:** creativity can be produced systematically. Generate many ideas through templates instead of free brainstorming, then filter.

### 2. Visual rhetoric typology (Phillips & McQuarrie, 2004, "Beyond Visual Metaphor", *Marketing Theory*)

- **Visual structures, by increasing complexity:**
  - juxtaposition (side by side);
  - fusion (merged into one image);
  - replacement (one element is absent and the other stands in its place; for example, a diamond standing for toothpaste).
- **Meaning operations:** connection or comparison, where comparison splits into similarity and opposition.
- **Refinement:** van Enschot, van Hooijdonk & Bronkhorst (2023) split replacement into three kinds:
  - source without target context;
  - source in target context;
  - target in source context.

  "Source in target context" scored highest on fluency and aesthetic pleasure ([Utrecht](https://research-portal.uu.nl/en/publications/refining-replacements-validating-a-revised-typology-of-visual-met/); [Tilburg](https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/publications/refining-replacements-validating-a-revised-typology-of-visual-met/)).
- **Partial counter-evidence:** Madupu, Sen & Ranganathan (2013), with 185 students, found juxtaposition drove more elaboration ([Marketing Management Journal](https://marketingmanagementjournal.scholasticahq.com/article/151065.pdf)).
- **Lesson for us:** the hero transformation of each film should be a fusion or replacement (the card becomes, or stands in for, the benefit's world). Keep juxtaposition for comparisons only.

### 3. Bisociation and conceptual blending

- **Koestler (1964), *The Act of Creation*:** creativity happens where two normally unrelated frames of thought ("matrices") intersect. The same pattern runs through art, science and humour.
- **Fauconnier & Turner, *The Way We Think* (2002):** blending builds a new "blended space" through three steps:
  - composition: relations visible only once inputs are combined;
  - completion: associations pulled in from each input;
  - elaboration: running the blend.

  Critics note it describes creative products better than where the inputs come from ([overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conceptual_blending); [Câmara Pereira on blending and creativity](https://eden.dei.uc.pt/~camara/files/QuestCRC.pdf)).
- **Lesson for us:** pick a deliberately distant second frame for the card (tide, orchestra, origami, constellation, bonsai, mint, garden, timetable). Keep a blend only if its emergent meaning is the product's proposition.

### 4. CRED's own creative strategy (trade press; treat figures with caution)

- **Approach:** CRED's TV work (with McCann's team, from 2020) deliberately builds curiosity and intrigue rather than explaining a layered product. It casts serious celebrities against type in absurd roles, launched in IPL ([afaqs: "the first CRED ad in 2020 said not everyone gets it"](https://www.afaqs.com/news/advertising/the-first-cred-ad-in-2020-said-not-everyone-gets-it-well-we-still-dont); [a copywriter's take](https://www.afaqs.com/news/guest-article/cred-not-everyone-gets-it-a-copywriters-take)).
- **Product films in the CRED Money style:** these do the opposite of the celebrity ads. They build one governing visual metaphor (money as an engraved world seen through a loupe) and route every feature through it.
- **Unverified claim:** one blog's "40% lower acquisition cost" is unverified and not to be cited.

### 5. Storyboarding motion: practical craft

- **Boards plot concepts, not every frame:** composition, shapes, movement and transitions, with camera moves marked by arrows and notes ([Artlist academy](https://artlist.io/academy/?p=1604); [Fable](https://www.fable.app/blog/how-do-you-storyboard-for-motion-graphics-motion-design)).
- **Match cuts carry visual metaphors:** keep the shared element in the same screen position across the cut, and mark the match point. Contrast cuts maximise difference ([Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/in/creativecloud/video/discover/match-cut.html); [Boris FX](https://borisfx.com/blog/what-is-a-match-cut/); [Film Lifestyle](https://filmlifestyle.com/match-cuts-creative-transitions/)).
- **Lesson for us:** every transition in a storyboard must name the shared element or metaphor it carries.

## 2026-10-10: second pass, research from the last two years (2024–2026)

The user's standing rule: the older work (Goldenberg 1999, Phillips & McQuarrie 2004) stays as a reference for storyboarding, but the recent findings below steer the choices. Tags: [Certain] abstract or primary page read, [Likely] secondary summary, [Guessing] inferred.

### 7. Which kind of visual metaphor to use, and when (Huang, 2024)

- **Paper:** "Visual metaphors in advertising: an interplay of visual structure, visual context, and conceptual tension", *Journal of Marketing Communications* 30(7), 762–791 ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/13527266.2023.2177708); [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/368484732_Visual_metaphors_in_advertising_An_interplay_of_visual_structure_visual_context_and_conceptual_tension)).
- **Finding** [Certain, abstract]: when the two ideas being joined are close (low conceptual tension), a **contextual fusion** (the fused object shown in a real context) is rated more artful and funnier and produces the best ad attitude. When the ideas are far apart (high tension), a **simple fusion** wins, because comprehension drops in the contextual version.
- **Rule for us:** our blends are mostly low-tension (card × chip, card × numerals, card × foil light), so build them as **contextual fusions**: the fused object lives inside a believable world (a city in the chip, a station for departures). For any far-fetched blend (card × constellation), keep it simple and uncluttered.

### 8. How much creativity, and what it must carry (Khan, Turner & Butt, 2024; Rosengren et al. 2020)

- **Khan et al.**, *Journal of Current Issues & Research in Advertising* 46(1), 2024 ([T&F](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/10641734.2024.2323992)) [Likely]: creativity moderates and **informativeness mediates** the effect of visual-metaphor ads. A striking metaphor that fails to inform underperforms.
- **Rosengren, Eisend, Koslow & Dahlen**, *Journal of Marketing* 2020 meta-analysis of 878 effect sizes ([SAGE](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0022242920929288)) [Certain]: creativity works best measured as **originality plus appropriateness**; originality alone has small effects on attitudes.
- **Rule for us:** every film must still read the four facts cleanly, muted, on first viewing. "Out of the box" never excuses an unreadable benefit. This is why the storyboards put the copy on screen for the whole beat.

### 9. Templates still beat brainstorming, for professionals (2024 working paper)

- "Creative performance in professional advertising development: the role of ideation templates, consumer insight, and intrinsic motivation" ([ResearchGate, 2024](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/385446104_Creative_performance_in_professional_advertising_development_The_role_of_ideation_templates_consumer_insight_and_intrinsic_motivation)) [Likely]: with industry judges, three templates (unification, metaphor, extreme consequence) raised originality **without hurting strategy**, versus a control.
- **Rule for us:** keep step 3 of the method (generate through templates, then filter). It is validated with professionals, not only students.

### 10. Emotion and "dull": the business case for craft (System1, Kantar, 2024–2025)

- **The Extraordinary Cost of Dull** (System1, eatbigfish, Peter Field; 2024 report, 2025 whitepaper) ([System1](https://system1group.com/the-extraordinary-cost-of-dull); [Peter Field whitepaper, Jan 2025](https://radiopub.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/01.27.25-Dullness-Of-Ads.pdf); [Mi3](https://www.mi-3.com.au/23-09-2024/adam-morgan-peter-field-system1-flip-ipa-effectiveness-database-count-cost-dull-ads)) [Likely, vendor research]: about half of TV ad responses are emotionally neutral (52% UK, 47% US; B2B 60%/54%). Dull ads need roughly **2–2.6× the media spend** for the same growth. "Showmanship" (drama, melody, a consistently used fluent device) drives feeling; salesmanship alone falls flat. Longer formats enable storytelling; short formats lean on melody and a recurring device.
- **Kantar / Affectiva** ([Kantar](https://www.kantar.com/Press-Center/Digital-ads-which-evoke-strong-emotions-are-four-times-more-likely-to-drive--brand-equity); [attention beyond views](https://www.kantar.com/inspiration/advertising-media/attention-beyond-views-for-creative-effectiveness)) [Likely]: ads that evoke strong emotion are about 4× more likely to drive long-term brand equity; completion and view metrics barely correlate with impact; **attention is necessary but insufficient**.
- **Rule for us:** each film needs one **fluent device** (a recurring motif: the loupe, the coin, the glowing line) and a **moment of delight** (the reveal, a surprise transformation), not just information. Music is part of the device, so choose it per concept.

### 11. What is winning craft awards now (2025)

- **Cannes Lions 2025 Film Craft Grand Prix:** Telstra "Better on a Better Network" (Bear Meets Eagle On Fire; director Jeff Low; animator Tobias Fouracre): 26 stop-motion films, real rural voices, absurdist warmth ([Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/cannes-lions-grand-prix-winners-2025/); [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/creativity/telstras-cinematic-spot-wins-cannes-lions-film-craft-grand-prix/); [shots](https://shots.net/news/view/cannes-lions-2025-film-craft-digital-craft-industry-craft-and-design-winners)) [Certain]. Lesson: **tactile material plus a human truth** beats polish.
- **D&AD 2025:** three Black Pencils, including FCB New York's "Spreadbeats" (Spotify), an animation coded inside an Excel spreadsheet, which also took Graphite Pencils in Animation for Motion Design ([Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/dad-black-pencil-winners-2025/); [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/creative-inspiration/d-and-ad-announces-2025-winners-including-3-coveted-black-pencils)) [Certain]. Lesson: **the medium itself is the idea** (an ad for a B2B audience made in the tool that audience lives in). For a card, the equivalent is building the film out of the card's own materials: chip, foil, emboss, receipt, guilloche.
- **Motion Awards 2025 (Motionographer):** winners include NOMINT's "In Hot Water" (WWF) nominated in experimental; commercial infographic and film-graphics winners ([winners](https://motionographer.com/2025/12/08/the-motion-awards-2025-winners/); [nominees](https://motionographer.com/2025/11/23/the-motion-awards-2025-nominees/)) [Certain that these pages exist; category detail partial].
- **Stash, Best of 2025 product films** ([Stash](https://www.stashmedia.tv/best-of-stash-2025-product-films/)) [Likely]: PUMA Speedcat, Beats Powerbeats Pro 2 "Behind the Design", Nike Air Max 1000. Common pattern: one product, macro material truth, continuous camera, exploded or transforming views.

### 12. Trend commentary for 2026 (treat as commentary, not data)

- Return to **narrative and emotional arcs** over quick flashy cuts; **hybrid 2D/3D**; **micro-dioramas** and oversized products in real environments; **tactile, imperfect, analog** textures as a counter-trend to AI gloss; AI for repetitive work with human taste for story ([Envato motion trends](https://elements.envato.com/learn/motion-design-trends); [Envato 3D trends](https://elements.envato.com/learn/3d-design-trends); [GarageFarm](https://garagefarm.net/blog/animation-trends-to-watch); [Genesis](https://genesismotiondesign.com/motion-design-trends-2026/)) [Guessing-to-Likely].
- **Rule for us:** our engraved, paper-grained, one-ink worlds sit squarely in the tactile counter-trend. Lean into dioramas (a whole world in one object) and hybrid 2D/3D (flat print becoming 3D), which is exactly the device the user liked in v2.

### 13. AI and ideation (2024–2025), relevant to how we generate ideas

- Generative models now score at or above humans on divergent-thinking tests (Hubert et al., *Scientific Reports* 2024 ([Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-53303-w)); Arora et al. 2025), but **feasibility and appropriateness** can be worse, and relying on AI output causes **design fixation** and **reduced collective diversity** (Wadinambiarachchi et al., CHI 2024; Doshi & Hauser, *Science Advances* 2024; [meta-analysis, 2025](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3746175.3746189)) [Likely].
- **Rule for us:** generate wide (30+), but filter hard on appropriateness and buildability, and deliberately pick distant frames to avoid converging on the same safe idea (as happened with the night films).
