## Rules
- Strict duotone per world. PCA on RGB pixels gives a first component of 96.7–99.6% in every shot, and each world has its own 2-colour axis: lighthouse #354e2c→#c9eab8, reef #091118→#dbe6df, bill #0c2a14→#e3f3cb, end card #1a5827→#fff8db. Saturated colour is reserved for foil, pearl/hero, UI cards and copy: 3–15% of pixels with S>90.
- The engraving is a straight, uniform diagonal line-screen (about 45°, rising '/') laid over rendered objects. It has the same angle on every surface and does NOT follow contours. Measured with FFT and directional autocorrelation on 64x64 patches: scene plates ≤0.55%H period (at Nyquist in a 640x360 source), banknote art 1.05%H (about 11 px at 1080p), water as horizontal lines at 1.45%H.
- Plates are 'living stills': a constant drift of 0.2–1.5°/s roll, ≤0.6%/s scale and ≤0.4%W/s pan (SIFT-tracked). Motion comes from subjects instead: rotating beams, fish at about 15%W/s, a foreground koi at 105–140%W/s, sliding cards.
- One real 3D camera move per scene, placed only after the copy has landed (about 1.9 s after title 2): a crane-up with tilt-down of 1.38 s. Ease-in 0.67 s with a near-linear velocity ramp, a short plateau at a median flow of about 15%W/s, then a 0.38 s ease-out.
- Hard cuts are cut-ins on the same subject, 1.6x (pearl 28→44 px) to 2.5x (tower shaft 27→72 px), that either mirror the hero to the other third (32%W→65%W, with the copy flipping right→left) or re-centre it (67%W→49.4%W). The title layer either swaps at the cut or persists across it in the same screen position.
- Wipe transition: a hard vertical edge moving right to left over 10 frames. Per-frame edge position is 100/97/89/81/73/65/53/35/23/13/4/0 %W. Both plates stay static (it reveals, it does not push), and the maximum-velocity frame (18%W/frame) sits on the bass hit.
- Loupe grammar. Iris-in takes 6–8 frames. The lens is a black circle of radius 48–50%W centred at (51%W, 53–55%H), feather 5–6%W, with no barrel distortion. Scenes change by a reusable 'loupe swap' rig, timed relative to the landing frame L: dip +20–25%H at L−17..L−10, hold at L−10..L−8, return at L−7..L−5, fling up-right at L−4..L−2 (through y≈−35%H), new lens enters from about (46%W, 80%H) at L−1, settled at L. The new content then relaxes with an exponential pull-back of 2.6–7% (τ≈10–12 frames). The rig was used 3 times at about 1.33 s spacing (47.04, 48.375, 49.708).
- Rhythm accelerates into the logo. Shot lengths in this segment are 3.21 s (lighthouse wide, full), 3.54 s, 1.71 s and 6.33 s (5 internal beats about 0.8–2.2 s apart), then 1.33, 1.33 and ≥1.33 s under the loupe.
- Copy system: one lowercase high-contrast serif headline per scene, x-height 4.4–5.0%H (font size about 9–10%H), plus an optional lowercase geometric-sans subline, x-height 1.7–1.9%H, 1–2%H below it. Blocks alternate bottom-right (centred at 68%W) → bottom-left (5%W) → top-centre (21–30%H). Each copy holds 3.2–4.0 s.
- Copy motion. A slow left-to-right sheen or colour band runs across the headline the whole time it is on screen, at 7–17%W/s. Entrance: a centre-out mask reveal over 4–5 frames, synced to an on-screen action (the shell slam), plus a 2.5%H rise with ease-out over about 24 frames; the subline follows 3 frames later. Exits: a left-to-right staggered dissolve over about 26 frames, or a 5-frame fade during a lens move. Otherwise the copy is cut away by a cut or a wipe. Copy sits under the lens vignette but above the world, and stays screen-locked during camera moves (≤2 px drift).
- UI cards are motivated by the world. The reminder card spawns inside the light beam on the exact frame the beam faces camera (lantern luminance peak, 36.83 s), then decelerates with an exponential ease-out over about 24 frames (left edge 146→42 px at 640).
