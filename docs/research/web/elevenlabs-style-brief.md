# ElevenLabs short launch videos: style brief for Remotion templates

**Before you build on this:** I could not watch any of these videos, and I found no source that describes them shot by shot. The durations, dates, aspect ratios, brand rules, typography and the orb/waveform code below come from sources. Almost everything about how text and shots move is my inference and is marked **[inferred]**. Scrub two or three of the reference videos frame by frame before you lock the timings (checklist in §7).

Access notes: WebFetch was blocked for every domain this session, so pages were read with TinyFish (11 of 15 calls used). TinyFish could not get YouTube transcripts, channel listings, full video descriptions or YouTube durations. Video durations came from the X posts' metadata through the `api.fxtwitter.com/i/status/<id>` mirror.

## 2. Visual language

### Brand system [sourced]
- **Agency:** basement.studio did the 2025 rebrand. In their words: "The logotype stayed, but everything else was rebuilt. Color, motion, composition… Inspired by Chladni patterns, our team created a suite of sound-wave visuals layered with blur, designed to feel atmospheric and sensorial. We even built a custom generator…" ([basement](https://basement.studio/showcase/elevenlabs-visual-rebrand-for-the-voice-of-ai.md)).
- **Platform sub-systems** ([elevenlabs.io/brand](https://elevenlabs.io/brand)). The brand page gives no hex values for these.

| Platform | Primary colour | Graphic motifs |
|---|---|---|
| ElevenAgents | Blue, with extra shades | "Image-based gradients with analog, tactile textures". Voice orbs and code-generated visuals. Main motif is the **circle/sphere**. |
| ElevenCreative | Orange, with extra shades | Variations of Chladni patterns. |
| ElevenAPI | Monochrome: black, white, neutrals | Chladni patterns plus **kinetic typography**. |
| ElevenMusic | Not stated | Has its own logotype. |

- **Logo rules** ([brand page](https://elevenlabs.io/brand)):
  - The symbol is the **"11" icon**: two vertical bars that also reference a **pause icon**.
  - Clear space around the logo equals the logo's height. The icon's holding shape is 3× the icon height.
  - **Correction to the brief:** "IIElevenLabs (using capital I's to recreate the 11 icon)" is explicitly on the "what to avoid" list, as are "ELEVENLABS" and "Eleven Labs". Use the official SVG, never typed characters.
  - Platform names are single words: ElevenAgents, ElevenCreative, ElevenAPI.

### Typography
- **Display face [sourced]:** Waldenburg, from Kimera Foundry, designed by Michael Clasen. It is a **grotesque (sans-serif)**: "early rationalism of Akzidenz Grotesk with the calligraphic warmth of Univers", in 24 cuts ([Abduzeedo](https://abduzeedo.com/waldenburg-typeface-kimera-foundry-earns-its-oscar-moment)). One third-party token file calls it a serif ([shadcn](https://www.shadcn.io/design/elevenlabs/raw)); that is wrong.
- **Web usage, from third-party extractions [sourced, medium confidence]** ([shadcn](https://www.shadcn.io/design/elevenlabs/raw), [hagicode](https://design.hagicode.com/designs/elevenlabs/DESIGN.md)):
  - Display is Waldenburg at **weight 300 (Light)**. Sizes 64 / 48 / 36 px, line-height 1.05–1.17, tracking −1.92 px at 64 px (−0.03 em) and −0.96 px at 48 px (−0.02 em).
  - Body is **Inter** 400/500 with +0.14 to +0.18 px tracking.
  - WaldenburgFH 700 in **uppercase** at 14 px with +0.7 px tracking appears on some buttons.
  - Code uses **Geist Mono**.
- **Ignore [low confidence]:** a "lime #9fe870 accent, Inter 700" claim on [webdesignhot](https://www.webdesignhot.com/design.md/elevenlabs/) contradicts every other source.

### Colour and surfaces [sourced, third-party extraction]
- Light canvas #f5f5f5 / #ffffff, ink #0c0a09 or #000.
- Body text #4e4e4e. Warm grey #777169. Warm stone #f5f2ef.
- Dark surfaces #0c0a09 and #1c1917. Hairline #e7e5e4.
- Pastel orb stops, used only as atmosphere: mint #a7e5d3, peach #f4c5a8, lavender #c8b8e0, sky #a8c8e8, rose #e8b8c4.
- Very soft layered shadows, for example `0 0 0 0.5px rgba(0,0,0,.075) inset` and the warm `0 6px 16px rgba(78,50,23,.04)`.
- Radii: pill CTAs 9999 px, cards 16 px, orb cards 24 px.

### The orb (exact, from ElevenLabs' open-source code) [sourced]
Source: [`orb.tsx`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/elevenlabs/ui/main/apps/www/registry/elevenlabs-ui/ui/orb.tsx), also shown on [21st.dev](https://21st.dev/@ElevenLabs/components/orb).

- **Geometry:** a three.js circle (radius 3.5, 64 segments) with a fragment shader.
- **Shapes:** 7 soft ovals laid out in polar coordinates, centred at 0, ½π … 3π. Each centre drifts by `0.5·sin(uTime/20 + offset)`.
- **Speed:** `uTime` advances 0.5 per second, so the drift period is about **251 s**. The orb barely moves.
- **Texture:** Perlin-noise "flow" distortion on the angle, plus two noisy **white rings** combined with a screen blend.
- **Colour:** the greyscale result is mapped through a ramp: **black → colour 1 → colour 2 → white**.
- **Default colours:** **#CADCFC / #A0B9D1**. Alternates are #F6E7D8 / #E0CFC2 and #E5E7EB / #9CA3AF. The widget docs use #6DB035 / #F5CABB as an example ([docs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-agents/customization/widget.md)).
- **Fade-in:** about 0.5 s.
- **States** (`t` is in seconds):
  - Idle: output level 0.3.
  - Listening: input `0.55 + 0.35·sin(3.2t)`.
  - Talking: input `0.65 + 0.22·sin(4.8t)`, output `0.75 + 0.22·sin(3.6t)`.
  - Levels are smoothed by 0.2 per frame and colours blend by 0.08 per frame.

### Waveforms and shimmer [sourced]
- **[Waveform](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/elevenlabs/ui/main/apps/www/registry/elevenlabs-ui/ui/waveform.tsx):** bars 4 px wide with a 2 px gap and 2 px radius, 128 px tall. Bar height is `max(4, v·h·0.8)` and opacity is `0.3 + 0.7·v`. Edges fade over 24 px.
- **Bar visualizer:** 15 bars at 20–100 % height, updated at 30 fps.
- **[ShimmeringText](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/elevenlabs/ui/main/apps/www/registry/elevenlabs-ui/ui/shimmering-text.tsx):** a 2 s linear sweep with a 0.5 s pause before repeating and a 0.3 s fade-in. Spread is 2 px per character. Base colour is muted and the highlight is the foreground colour.

## 3. Motion language
Mostly **[inferred]**. These are best guesses consistent with the brand system above.
- **Overall feel:** editorial and calm. Smooth expo-out deceleration with **no overshoot or bounce**. That fits the light 300-weight display type and the slow orb.
- **Text entrances:** word by word or line by line, either a mask reveal or a short blur-in (8 → 0 px blur, 0.3 em rise, 12–16 frames, 2–4 frame stagger). Headlines are three to six words.
- **Shot length:** a 40–50 s spot has about 10–14 shots averaging 3–4 s. Title cards hold 1.5–2.5 s, UI demos 4–6 s, the end card 2.5–3.5 s.
- **Camera:** slow push-ins of 1.00 → 1.05–1.08 scale over a shot. Parallax only between a UI card and the background.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts on the beat or soft blur dissolves of 8–12 frames.
- **Product UI:** floating rounded cards on a flat canvas, cropped tight and zoomed rather than shown in a full device frame. A cursor shows clicks and the prompt is typed live. The generated output is then *heard*: the VO or demo audio plays over a waveform.
- **Supporting evidence:** the motion-designer job asks for "motion to narrate user experience", After Effects and Figma, with Rive and Lottie as nice-to-haves, and work running from "script → storyboard → final videos" with the Video and Design teams ([careers](https://elevenlabs.io/careers/d6ce93b1-b4c9-40a6-afb5-fcc4570c610d/motion-designer)).
- **Studio 3.0:** freelancer David Akinlosotu *claims* he made the Studio 3.0 launch motion design, with 56M views ([Contra](https://contra.com/p/rA2bWvll-i-created-the-highly-rated)). I could not verify this; ElevenLabs credits no one. Joseph Aluko's Contra post is a recreation, not the original ([Contra](https://contra.com/joseph_aluko_yz4rajut/work)).

One post says "Background music for this video was created with Eleven Music" ([X](https://x.com/elevenlabsio/status/1958542409698029973)). - **[sourced]** The v3 launch was demonstrated with expressive voice samples using audio tags such as [excited], [whispers] and [sighs] ([blog](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/eleven-v3)).
- **[inferred]**
  - Voice-over uses ElevenLabs' own voices; the voice demo itself is the "hero".
  - Music is restrained electronic/ambient with a pulse at roughly 100–120 BPM, a riser into the end card, and soft UI clicks and whooshes.
  - Mix target: −14 LUFS integrated and −1 dBTP for YouTube and X. Duck the music 10–12 dB under the voice.

## 6. Scene recipes
For 30 fps. Sizes are given as vertical 1080×1920 / landscape 1920×1080. Easing names used below:
- **EXPO** = `Easing.bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`
- **INOUT** = `Easing.bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)`
- **QUINT** = `Easing.bezier(0.83,0,0.17,1)`

1. **Hook title [inferred]**
   - Background #0c0a09 for dark or #f5f5f5 for light.
   - Waldenburg 300 (or Inter Tight 300 as a stand-in) at **112 px / 128 px**, line-height 1.05, tracking −0.03 em, centred. Maximum width 900 px / 1500 px.
   - Each word: opacity 0 → 1, translateY 0.35 em → 0, blur 8 → 0 px over **14 f** EXPO, staggered **3 f**.
   - Hold 36 f, then exit with opacity → 0 and blur 6 px over 8 f, or cut on the beat.
2. **Orb hero (ElevenAgents) [sourced shader, inferred framing]**
   - Port `orb.tsx` to `@remotion/three` and make it deterministic: `uTime = frame/30·0.5`, and replace the per-frame smoothing with values computed from the frame.
   - Diameter **760 px / 620 px**, centred about 8 % above middle.
   - Colours #CADCFC / #A0B9D1, or a blue pair for Agents.
   - Fade in over 15 f. Scale 0.92 → 1.00 over 90 f EXPO.
   - Halo: radial gradient of colour 2 at 25 % alpha, 1.4× the radius, blurred 80 px.
   - Drive `manualOutput` from voice-over loudness (`visualizeAudio`, 32 samples) so the orb "talks" in sync.
3. **Chladni atmosphere (Creative / API) [sourced concept, inferred numbers]**
   - Shader: `f = cos(nπx)cos(mπy) − cos(mπx)cos(nπy)`. Draw lines where |f| < 0.02.
   - Morph (n, m) from (3, 5) to (4, 7) over 120 f INOUT.
   - Two layers: sharp lines in white at 35 % plus a copy blurred 32 px at 70 %.
   - Monochrome on #000, or tinted orange for Creative. Grain at 3 %.
4. **Floating UI card [inferred]**
   - White card on #f5f5f5, or #1c1917 on #0c0a09.
   - Radius **32 px**, shadows 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,.06) and 0 24px 48px rgba(0,0,0,.08).
   - Enter: y +60 → 0, scale 0.96 → 1 over **20 f** EXPO.
   - Camera push 1.00 → 1.06 across the shot.
   - Card width 960 / 1280 px.
5. **Cursor and click [inferred]**
   - Arrow cursor 44 px. Moves over **18–24 f** INOUT.
   - Click: cursor scales 0.85 for 4 f, ripple ring grows 0 → 64 px at opacity 0.4 → 0 over 12 f.
   - The pressed button darkens #292524 → #0c0a09.
6. **Prompt typing (Voice Design / Music / SFX) [inferred]**
   - Pill input, Inter 400 at **40 / 34 px**.
   - Typing speed 2 characters per frame. Caret blinks with a 16 f period.
   - Then an ink pill "Generate" button, **88 px** tall, label in Inter 500.
7. **Waveform playback [sourced component, values scaled ×2]**
   - Bars 8 px / gap 4 px / radius 4 px, height 256 px, edge fade 48 px, opacity 0.3 + 0.7·v. Feed from the real audio track.
   - Karaoke caption beneath: Inter 500 at 44 px. Current word white, the rest at 40 %.
   - Audio-tag chips such as `[whispers]` in Geist Mono 26 px on a #f0efed pill.
8. **Kinetic feature list (API) [sourced motif, inferred timing]**
   - Waldenburg 300 at **96 / 88 px**.
   - Line mask reveal: translateY 100 % → 0 over **16 f** EXPO, stagger 4 f, one line per beat (every 15–20 f).
   - Active line in full ink; earlier lines drop to #a8a29e.
9. **Stat card (e.g. "75ms", "#1") [inferred]**
   - Number counts up over **24 f** EXPO, Waldenburg 300 at **280 / 240 px**, tracking −0.04 em.
   - Unit in Inter 500 at 64 px, #777169.
   - Comparison bars grow over 20 f. The ElevenLabs bar is ink; the rest are #d6d3d1.
10. **Shimmer status line [sourced]**
    - "Generating…" in #777169 with a sweep to #fff (dark) or #0c0a09 (light).
    - **60 f** linear, then a 15 f pause before repeating. Spread 2 px per character.
11. **Montage grid (Music / Image & Video) [inferred]**
    - 3×4 / 4×3 grid of tiles, radius 24 px, gap 16 px.
    - Tiles scale 0.9 → 1 over 12 f EXPO with a 2 f stagger.
    - Cut the hero tile every 8–12 f on the beat.
12. **End card [sourced logo rules, inferred motion]**
    - Official SVG logo at **500 / 640 px** wide, clear space at least the logo height.
    - The two "11" bars grow scaleY 0 → 1 from the centre over **12 f** EXPO, 3 f apart. The wordmark then fades and un-blurs 10 → 0 px over 18 f.
    - Product logotype if needed (ElevenAgents etc.).
    - CTA "Try Eleven v4 →" in Inter 500 at **36 / 32 px**, #777169, placed 48 px below.
    - Hold **75 f**. Final music hit lands on the logo's frame.

Global settings **[inferred]**: transitions are a hard cut on the beat, or a blur dissolve over 10 f (A: blur 0 → 20 px and opacity → 0; B: the reverse). Vertical safe area: keep content inside a 960×1400 box with 220 px clear at the top and 380 px at the bottom.

**Checklist for scrubbing videos 1, 7 and 9:**
- Light or dark canvas.
- Whether headlines are Waldenburg Light.
- Frames per word in text entrances.
- Number of shots and average shot length.
- Whether the cursor and device frames appear.
- Orb or Chladni usage.
- End-card hold length and CTA text.
- Where cuts land relative to the beat.
