# From raw script to storyboard: the director's pass

Use this when someone hands over a video script, an ad brief or a feature list. Work through the four
passes in order and write the result into the spec. Show your thinking to the user as the table from
pass 2, not as prose.

## Pass 1. Extract the claims (what must be said)

Read the whole script once, then list every factual benefit, word for word.

- **Keep** concrete, checkable claims: "5% rewards on online shopping", "zero joining fee",
  "redeem on flights, hotels".
- **Drop** filler and adjectives ("revolutionary", "seamless") unless the user insists. They are tone, not claims.
- **Mark** anything that looks unverified (an unlabelled number, "best in India") and ask the user before
  putting it on screen.
- **Keep 2–5 claims.** If there are more, rank them by what a customer would act on, and ask which to cut.
  Each claim needs about 5 seconds: three claims make a good 24-second film, five make about 35 seconds.
- **Split each claim:**
  - **headline:** the 1–4 words a viewer remembers (`5% rewards`);
  - **sub-line:** the rest of the exact wording (`on online shopping`).

## Pass 2. One metaphor per claim (how it will look)

For each claim, ask: "What single object would make someone feel this claim without reading it?" Then
pick from the subject library, or note a new model to source for the bespoke pass.

| Claim type | Metaphor directions that work | Library subjects |
|---|---|---|
| Rewards, cashback, points | something that grows or blooms; a sharp animal that finds the good stuff | `plant`, `flowers`, `fox` |
| Zero / no fee, free | a treasure that costs nothing to open; a pearl from a shell | `shell` (hero) + `fish` |
| Fast, instant, one tap | something running or racing | `fox-run`, `car` |
| Travel, flights, hotels | a light that guides the way; vehicles | `lantern`, `car` (plus a 2D globe in the bespoke pass) |
| Shopping, store, products | a still life of real products on display | `shoe`, `boombox`, `bottle`, `avocado` |
| Security, trust | a lantern in the dark; something sheltered | `lantern` |
| Freedom, flexibility | a fish swimming; open water | `fish` + `sea` palette |

Rules:
- **The hero carries the claim.** The first subject is the hero (centre, foil); 1–2 supporting subjects
  give it context.
- **Prefer the metaphor to the literal.** A shell for "zero fee" beats a coin with a zero on it.
- **Never repeat a hero** across claims.

Write this table into your reply:

| # | Headline / sub | Metaphor (one line: why it fits) | Subjects | Palette | Camera | Enters by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Pass 3. Order, colour and pacing (the shape of the film)

- **Order:**
  - Lead with the claim most people care about; a big number or "zero" works well first or last.
  - Put the product/brand claim near the start if it names the product.
  - End on the strongest claim before the outro.
- **Colour:** one duotone per world, never the same palette twice in a row. Pick by feeling:
  - green: growth, rewards
  - sea: calm, free
  - violet: premium, fast
  - terra: warm, shopping
  - slate: serious, security
  - indigo: travel, night
  - cream: heritage
- **Camera:** vary the camera between claims: push (focus), orbit (show off an object), crane (reveal a
  place).
- **Transitions:** vary them too. Split suits a calm reveal, wipe keeps momentum, dive suits a "magical"
  claim, iris opens a new chapter.
- **Length:** 24 s for 3 claims, 30 s for 4, 35 s for 5. Set `seconds` in the spec; the planner rounds to
  whole beats.
- **Outro:**
  - `title` is the product's full name;
  - `rows` are the claims in short form, in film order (3–4 rows);
  - `screen` is `dark` for premium or fintech products, `light` for friendly consumer products.
- **Lockup:** the brand's official logo file if you have it, else the name in type. `line` is the product
  line.

## Pass 4. Sound (the planner does this, check it)

The planner composes a score on a 120 BPM grid and places SFX on every visual event:
- pen swishes on the opening;
- a whoosh into each transition;
- an impact on each world;
- a pop per subject;
- a sparkle on each headline;
- a click and ticks in the outro;
- a riser into the lockup.

Change `key` for mood: D and E are bright, A is warm, F# is airier.

## Worked example: CRED IndusInd Bank RuPay card

| # | Headline / sub | Metaphor | Subjects | Palette | Camera | Enters by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5% rewards / on online shopping | a sharp fox finding the best in a garden that blooms | fox, flowers | green | push | iris |
| 2 | redeem on flights, hotels / and 2,000+ products on CRED store | a lantern lighting the way, then a still life of products | lantern, car | indigo | crane | split |
| 3 | zero joining fee | a pearl-like treasure that costs nothing to open | shell, fish | sea | orbit | dive |

Outro: dark screen, title "CRED IndusInd Bank RuPay credit card", rows = the three claims. The lockup uses
the official logo file and the line "IndusInd Bank RuPay credit card".

The bespoke v6.4 then upgraded each world:
- the garden became the card's print;
- the strip split open into a globe with flight arcs;
- the globe matched the colonnade floor;
- ₹0 rose from the shell.
