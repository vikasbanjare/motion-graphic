# Specialised Guideline 07: Short 5-15 Second Motion Ad

A paid or organic social spot of **5, 6, 10 or 15 seconds** that does one job: stop the thumb, prove one thing, and leave the brand and one action in the viewer's head. It is not a cutdown made by trimming a longer film (Part 08 §17.5 rule 1 forbids that), not a feature walkthrough (Guideline 02) and not a launch film (Guideline 01). It is its own format with its own arithmetic: at 15 s there is room for **one transaction**, at 10 s for **one action**, at 5-6 s for **one claim and the logo**.

This guideline condenses the Master SaaS Motion Design System (Parts 01-11 in `research/master/`) and the eight frame-measured teardowns in `research/videos/` into one working spec for this format. Every number is in frames at 30 fps (f) unless stated; 1 f = 33.3 ms. "% W" and "% H" are percent of frame width and height; pixel values are for 1080×1920 (9:16) unless marked "@1080" (16:9 at 1920×1080). Numbers are measured in the references unless marked [inferred] (derived by this guideline or by the master from measured rules, not observed directly).

**Evidence warning, read first.** The shortest reference is Chowdeck at 17.97 s; no 5, 6 or 10 s film was measured. The 15 s plan is anchored on Chowdeck's measured structure. The 5 and 10 s plans are the master's extrapolations (Part 08 PL-05, PL-10; Part 08 DU-X3 lists them as "validate before using as a default"). Treat every 5-10 s number as a reasoned starting point and A/B it.

## 1. Purpose and audience

**Purpose.** A short ad buys one of three outcomes, and the runtime decides which one you can afford:

| Runtime | Job it can do | What it cannot do |
|---|---|---|
| **5-6 s** (bumper, sting, pre-roll, end slate) | **Recall.** One claim (≤ 5 words) or one state change, then the brand registered for ≥ 1.5 s | Explain the product, show a flow, carry narration (10 spoken words leave no time for the lockup, Part 08 §17.3) |
| **10 s** (teaser, story ad, micro-demo) | **Curiosity + one proof.** Hook, name, one action and its result, lockup with a verb CTA | Show range, carry more than one benefit |
| **15 s** (performance ad, app-install ad, vertical cutdown) | **Conversion.** One complete transaction (need → action → result → brand → ask), the structure Chowdeck measures [V:126cpH] | Teach a multi-step workflow or several features (use Guideline 02 or 05) |

**The four duties, in order** (all runtimes):
1. **Stop the scroll by frame 0.** The hook is on the first frame and changes by f3 (Part 01 H1; Part 11 defaults #24). Frame 0 is also the thumbnail and the autoplay preview (Part 01 H7).
2. **Land the name or the answer by 3 s** (Part 01 H6). Chowdeck says "order in seconds" at 2.53 s; Bumper says "PRO" at 1.17 s.
3. **Prove one thing** with a visible state change, held still for ≥ 1.0 s (Part 08 SD-17, ER-16).
4. **Register the brand and the action:** a lockup still for ≥ 1.5 s with the music resolving on it, and a verb CTA on any paid placement (Part 01 L1, L4). Chowdeck's lack of a CTA is a flagged flaw [V:126cpH flaw 4].

**Audience.**

| Viewer | Where they meet it | State of mind | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold prospects | TikTok In-Feed, Reels, Shorts, Stories, YouTube pre-roll | Thumb moving, sound off on most feeds, 1.7 s mobile dwell before a swipe (Part 08 SD-01, [N]) | Text on f0, sound-off safe, the claim in their own words and idiom |
| Warm retargeting audiences | Meta, YouTube, LinkedIn feeds | Already visited; need a reason to act now | Lead with the outcome number or the offer; CTA with a verified offer |
| Existing users | Stories, the brand's own channel | Know the product | A 6-10 s "now with X" teaser, then route to Guideline 05 |
| Brand / channel viewers | YouTube bumper, event screens, end slates | Passive, often sound on | 5-6 s sting: one image, one line, the logo |

**The promise.** "In the time you've watched this, you've seen it work." A short ad that shows a flow the product cannot do, a result it cannot reach or an offer that has expired spends more trust than it earns. Every number and offer must be real (Part 11; kit recipe copy tips: "never invent the proof number, the price or the deadline").

## 2. When to choose this format

Choose a 5-15 s motion ad when **all** of these hold:

1. The placement is a feed, Stories, a bumper slot or an end slate, where attention is bought second by second.
2. The value fits in **one sentence of ≤ 6 words** and **one visible state change** (a tap → a result, a before → after, a number moving).
3. There is a single action you want the viewer to take (install, try, book, visit), or the goal is pure recall.
4. You can produce or rebuild the one product moment truthfully (real UI, rebuilt UI or a real product shot).

| Situation | This format? | Better alternative |
|---|---|---|
| App-install or sign-up performance ad | **Yes, 15 s** (PL-15) | — |
| Pre-launch teaser, "coming Monday" | **Yes, 10 s** (PL-10) | — |
| YouTube bumper, event sting, channel ident | **Yes, 5-6 s** (PL-05) | — |
| A/B hook testing for a longer campaign | **Yes, 15 s**, varying shot 1 only (Part 08 §17.5 rule 4) | — |
| Cutdown of an existing 30-60 s film | **Yes**, by removing whole blocks and re-timing to PL-15/PL-10 (§4.5) | Never by trimming every shot |
| One new feature for existing users | 10-15 s teaser here, full film in Guideline 05 | Guideline 05 |
| A workflow with 3+ steps, a platform tour, onboarding | No | Guideline 02 (UI demo) or a 30-60 s film |
| Two or more benefits that both need proof | No | 30 s (PL-30) |
| A VO-led explanation | No (narration needs 150-170 wpm; 15 s holds ≈30-37 words at most, with no room for the lockup) | 30 s+ VO explainer (PL-90E) |
| No product moment can be shown truthfully | Only as a 5-6 s brand sting (PL-05 variant B) | — |

**Master order** [inferred from Part 11 EX-U10]: build the **9:16 master first** for this format (most short-ad inventory is vertical), then re-lay out (never crop) to 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9. Keep every duration identical in milliseconds; reposition every string inside the new format's safe area; scale travel distances as % of the frame in the direction of travel (Part 03 §18.11). A statement that breaks onto more lines in a narrower frame moves up a reading tier, so add 0.3-0.5 s to that shot and take it from the neighbouring proof shot (Part 08 §17.5 rule 3).

### 3.2 Length rules

| Length | Frames @30 | Shots (calm / balanced / fast) | Scene changes | Bars at 120 BPM | Words on screen (whole ad) | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 s | 150 | 2 / 2 / 3 | 2-3 | 2.5 | ≤ 10 | Part 08 §17.3 [inferred] |
| 6 s (bumper) | 180 | 3 | 2-3 | 3 | ≤ 12 | PL-05 + 1.0 s, §4.2 [inferred] |
| 10 s | 300 | 4 / 5 / 6 | 4-6 | 5 | ≤ 18 | PL-10 [inferred] |
| 15 s | 450 | 6 / 7 / 9 | 6-9 | 7.5 | ≤ 25 | PL-15; Chowdeck ≈25 words incl. UI statuses [V:126cpH] |

- **Never shorten the ASL because the film is short** (Part 10 §11.6; Part 08 ER-04). A 15 s ad cuts at ASL ≈1.67 s, the same temperament as Chowdeck's 1.63 s, not faster.
- **Deliver exactly the slot length.** A 6 s bumper that runs 6.2 s is rejected or cut; a 15 s ad that runs 15.8 s loses its lockup tail. End the sting tail on the last frame (Part 09 §16.13.2: the tail decays to ≤ −60 dB by the final frame).
- **No speech under 10 s** unless it is the product's own audio (Part 08 §17.3). At 15 s, a VO line of ≤ 12 words may sit over the hook and the CTA only.

### 3.3 The silent loop (website, in-app, GIF)

A 6-10 s version with no audio that loops: frame 0 and the last frame share the background colour and the anchor element's position (±1 px @1080, Part 11 defaults #6); the lockup still is shortened to 1.0 s and followed by a 10-15 f return move into the hook's first pose [inferred]. No text may be cut mid-read at the loop point.

## 4. Story structure with exact beat timings

All plans are drawn on a **120 BPM grid**: 1 beat = 0.5 s = 15 f, 1 bar = 2.0 s = 60 f (Part 08 §17.4.0). It is frame-exact at 24, 30 and 60 fps. When the real track has another tempo, re-snap grid cuts with BS-04 (t = t0 + n × 60 / BPM, round, lead by 0-2 f); no section boundary moves more than one beat. "Grid" cuts land 0-2 f before the listed frame (picture leads); "free" cuts follow the product's own timing.

### 4.1 Stage maps

| Stage | 5 s (PL-05) | 6 s bumper [inferred] | 10 s (PL-10) | 15 s (PL-15) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0.0-1.5 · f0-45 · 30% | 0.0-1.5 · f0-45 · 25% | 0.0-1.5 · f0-45 · 15% | 0.0-1.5 · f0-45 · 10% |
| Setup / problem | merged into the hook | merged | merged | 1.5-2.5 · f45-75 · 6.7% |
| Reveal (name + promise) | merged into the lockup | merged | 1.5-3.0 · f45-90 · 15% | 2.5-4.5 · f75-135 · 13.3% |
| Proof | 1.5-3.0 · f45-90 · 30% | 1.5-3.5 · f45-105 · 33% | 3.0-6.0 · f90-180 · 30% | 4.5-10.5 · f135-315 · 40% |
| Climax / payoff | inside the proof | inside the proof | 6.0-7.5 · f180-225 · 15% | 10.5-12.0 · f315-360 · 10% |
| Resolution (CTA + lockup) | 3.0-5.0 · f90-150 · 40% | 3.5-6.0 · f105-180 · 42% | 7.5-10.0 · f225-300 · 25% | 12.0-15.0 · f360-450 · 20% (CTA 1.0 s + lockup 2.0 s) |

Why the lockup share grows as the runtime shrinks: the lockup needs ≥ 1.5 s still in every runtime (ER-U9), so it is 40% of a bumper and 20% of a 15 s ad. A bumper's job is recall, so that is the right spend (PL-05 "why it works").

**Variants of the 15 s plan** (keep the times; Part 08 PL-15):

### 4.5 Cutting down from a longer film

Remove whole blocks; never trim every shot (Part 08 §17.5 rule 1). From a 30-60 s film keep: hook → reveal → **the single strongest proof block** → payoff → CTA → lockup, then re-time them to PL-15 or PL-10. The cut's ASL must stay within ±10% of the hero film's (Part 08 §17.8 item 11). Re-edit the music on bar lines for the new length; never fade the track out early (Part 11 EX-U10).

(Part 09 §16.9.3, [inferred] for these runtimes.) Burst-and-breathe cycles of 2-4 s, with ≈1 visual event per 0.7 s overall [V:126cpH rule 15].

## 6. Style direction

### 6.1 Visual

**Pick one style and keep it** (Part 01 TS1). Styles that work at 5-15 s (Part 10 §11.6):

| Style (Part 10) | Best for | Short-ad signature |
|---|---|---|
| **ST-G Playful Illustrated Collage + UI** (measured 15-20 s, 9:16) | Consumer apps, fintech, delivery, warm SaaS | Dialect hook, chapter background colours, flat → real swaps, tossed UI card, one transaction |
| **ST-E2 Dark Neon Reel** | Dev tools, asset libraries, "drops" | Zero-copy icon hook, zoom-through, punch cards 10-12 f, snap-hold-suck |
| **ST-A2 Prompt-Native** | AI features, connectors | Typed line → UI → lockup (works down to 5-6 s as a typed line → lockup) |
| **ST-C1 Brand-Bookended Montage** (cut) | Platforms with range | Macro hook → anchored montage (inserts 0.5-1.3 s) → logo |
| **ST-F2 Monochrome Brand System** | Bumpers, agencies | One shape chain → logo |
| Avoid at this length | ST-B Cinematic, ST-C2 How-To, ST-D 3D Tech film, ST-H Live-action story | Need setup time a short ad does not have |

**Composition rules for 9:16** (Part 02 defaults; Part 06 §8.12):
- Statement frames: ≥ 85% negative space; statement centred on y 47-52% H, ≤ 85% W.
- Hero UI card ≈75% W (810 px), its text-bearing rows inside x 120-840.
- Hero object (product, illustration) 30-45% W.
- Text-level ratio inside one frame ≥ 1 : 2.5 (setup word : hero word 1 : 2.5-3).
- Notifications and status pills at ≤ 63% H (≤ 1210 px). Chowdeck's pill at ≈70% H sits under the caption overlay.
- Nothing that carries meaning in the top 270 px or the bottom 710 px.
- Device frames are optional and justified only for mobile apps ("this is the app").

### 6.2 Color

- **Named colours: ≤ 5 per film, ≤ 4 per frame** (excluding real UI and photos) (Part 02 defaults). Chowdeck uses 6 with ≤ 4 per frame [V:126cpH].
- **One brand accent**, split into a field tone and a text tone (CL-U3). Accent area ≤ 5% of the frame when it is a highlight; a full field only as a chapter background.
- **Chapter colour coding** [V:126cpH rule 3]: the anchor brand colour opens and closes the ad (bookends); change the background per chapter, ≤ 4 backgrounds per 15-18 s.
- **Canvas:** light off-white #F5F5F5-#F9FAFC (warm #F9F5F2) or hue-tinted near-black (#06004E, #0C0A09, #161616); never pure #000 without dither.
- **Contrast:** ≥ 4.5:1 for all text; 3:1 only for text ≥ 66 px on a 1080-wide 9:16 frame; 7:1 or a ≥ 60% scrim over footage (Part 02 defaults; Part 07 #22).
- **Light:** one key-light direction for the whole ad, shared by 2D shadows and any 3D (Part 10 defaults #21). Glow only on emitters and "active" states, ≤ 2-3% of cap height on type.
- **Gradients:** 1-2% dither on every gradient and dark frame before the 8-bit encode (Part 11 defaults #20); banding is the most visible compression failure in feeds.

Palette presets that suit short ads (contrast-verified in Part 02 §11.13): Dark neon (#0A0F0A canvas, #F5F7F5 ink, #38D037 accent); Night/Day (#06004E / #FCFCFC, #F4643C accent); Prompt-native (#FFFFFF, #1A1A1A, #F65542 icon only, #CE4737 accent text).

### 6.3 Typography

| Role | 9:16 (1080×1920) | 16:9 (@1080) | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero word (T-HERO) | **240 px font**; ascender 12-13% H; ≈84% W | 185 px font (cap 12% H) | Hook and payoff only; ≤ 2 words |
| Statement (T-STATEMENT) | 128 px font | 90 px font (cap 5.8% H) | ≤ 6 words, ≤ 32 characters |
| Setup word over a hero word | ≈1/3 of the hero size (≈80 px) | ≈60 px | Ratio 1 : 2.5-3 |
| CTA | cap ≥ 5% H (≥ 96 px cap, ≈135 px font) | cap ≥ 5% H (≥ 54 px cap) | 1-3 words, ≤ 16 characters |
| URL / handle | ≥ 3.5% H (≥ 67 px) | ≥ 3.5% H (≥ 38 px) | Lowercase domain, no "https://" |
| Must-read UI text | font ≥ 52 px (floor 36 px) | cap ≥ 3% H (≈46 px font) | Below 2.5% H it is texture only |

- **Families:** one sans for all copy plus at most one accent face with one role. Weight 500-600 for launch energy, 800-900 for playful consumer, 400 for calm premium.
- **Tracking:** −1 to −3% on lowercase display; 0 to +3% on display caps. **Leading:** 0.95-1.1 on display; Devanagari 1.25 headline / 1.5 body.
- **Overshoot on type: 0%** (8/8 references).
- **Hindi / Hinglish:** fonts that carry both scripts (Poppins, Mukta, Anek Devanagari, Hind; display Baloo 2, Teko); load the `devanagari` subset explicitly; animate by word or orthographic syllable, never by code point; reading times × 1.15 (Part 07 §9.19).

## 8. Camera language

**Budget for 5-15 s** (Part 04 §6.4, [inferred] below 15 s):

| Runtime | Breathing | Motivated moves | Signature moves | 3D / DOF beats | Punches / whips |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5-6 s | Every shot | 1 | 0-1 | 0 | 1 |
| 10 s | Every shot | 1-2 | 1 | 0 | 1-2 |
| 15 s | Every shot | 2-4 | 1 | 0-1 | 2 |

**Rules**
- **One signature move per ad** (CM-U10). Chowdeck's is the tilt through clouds; pick one (tilt, zoom-through, pull-out, push-through) and do not repeat it.
- **Prefer vertical and scale moves in 9:16** (Part 04 §6.5): vertical push (1 frame height in ≈11 f, expo-in-out, ≈10% H directional blur at the peak), two-stage pull-out, upward pan, pedestal tilt. Keep any single lateral move ≤ 50% W.
- **Breathing on every hold:** +10% scale across a 1.5-2 s hold, linear (≈0.16-0.21%/f), or 0.1-0.2% W/f drift.
- **Read-safe speed:** ≤ 0.2% W/f while text is read.
- **Cut-ins instead of zooms:** 2-3.5× instant, to make one UI value readable (Part 04 defaults #5). A cut-in is faster and crisper than an animated zoom [V:15VhHR rule 13].
- **Roll 0°, overshoot 0%** on the camera (8/8).
- **Seamless lockup move:** 30 f S-curve tilt/pedestal through a horizontal layer + ≈10 f settle; the logo fades in over the last 8 f while riding up ≈23% H to its rest position [V:126cpH rule 11].
- **End every move inside the safe area.** Reels UI alone covers the top 14% and bottom 35% of the 9:16 height [N]; the strict cross-platform band leaves out the top 14% + bottom 37% (≈51%): keep meaning inside y 270-1210 of 1920 (P02 §4.7).
- **3D:** at most one beat of 1.4-2.5 s in a 15 s ad (≈10-15% of runtime), and only when it explains the product better than flat UI; none in 5-10 s ads.

|---|---|---|

| CM-03 punch into a cut | +18-33% in 3-8 f, E-PUNCH | Hook exit, climax exit |
| CM-05 pull-back reveal | ×0.75-0.80 over 6-31 f (2D); two-stage ≈×0.25 in ≈1 s for a range reveal | Reveal, proof context |

| CM-18 rack focus | 8-12 f ease-out from a blur of ≈8-10% W | Morph hand-offs, live-action reveal |

## 9. Transitions

**Budget** (Part 05 §7.5):

| Runtime | Scene changes | Signature device uses | Hero one-off transitions | Shape/mask wipes | Flash moments |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 s | 2-3 | 1-2 | 0-1 | 0 | 0 |
| 10 s | 4-6 | 2-3 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| 15 s | 6-9 | 3 | 1-2 | ≤ 1 | 0 |

**Rules**
- **≥ 70% hard cuts** (plain or with a designed hand-off).
- **Pre-roll into a cut:** the last 6-10 f accelerate (expo-in, ×1.3-1.9 per frame). **Settle out of a cut:** expo-out, 40-64% of travel on frame 1.
- **Beat offset** (music-led sections): cut 0-2 f **before** the beat, never after. Chowdeck's 5 hard cuts land on 0 onsets; the edit is narrative-led, and only the click and the lockup are synced. For a music-led short ad, aim for ≥ 60% of hard cuts within ±2 f of the grid.
- **Action → consequence:** the cut or state change 3-15 f after the press (median ≈6-8 f).
- **No flash frames** under 30 s (budget 0); if one is ever used: ≤ 3 f per colour, ≤ 3 flashes/s (WCAG 2.3.1).
- **Overshoot on transition elements 0%** in premium styles (Chowdeck's card ≈25% is a playful-style exception).

| Transition | Spec | Best place in a short ad |
|---|---|---|
| TR-01 hard cut | 0 f; 1 f empty plate in the incoming colour allowed | Most changes |
| TR-03 match cut | Matched element within ±5% W of its position | Hook → setup (graphic match on the hero word's shape or colour) |
| TR-07 UI morph (shared element) | ≈7-13 f, background re-focused over 11-12 f; the anchor never leaves the screen | Action → progress (card → toast/row) |
| TR-08 whip | 6-9 f expo-in, peak 13-20% W/f, blur on the last 2 f | Once at most |
| TR-11 zoom-through | 3 f ease-in, then ×1.36/f | Icon hook → product |
| TR-14 light bridge | White bloom 3-6 f; colour wash 7-18 f | Reveal on dark styles |
| TR-17 seamless camera move | 30 f S-curve + ≈10 f settle | Payoff → lockup |
| TR-18 polarity flip | 1 f colour inversion on the same layout | Claim → answer in kinetic styles |
| TR-20 interaction-triggered | The tap/click causes the change; the cut 3-8 f after the press | The proof |
| Vertical push | 11 f, expo-in-out, ≈10% H directional blur on both layers | Setup → reveal ("forward" in 9:16) |
| TR-24 dissolve | Avoid; crossfades read as filler at this length | — |

**Velocity hand-off:** keep the incoming motion within 0.5-2× the outgoing speed and in the same screen direction (Part 05 §7.3.2), or the cut feels like a jump.

## 10. UI treatment

### 10.1 Believability in 15 s

- **One flow, one screen, one result.** A short ad has time for exactly one UI action (SD-12) and one result (SD-17). A second screen is allowed only as the consequence of the first (a morph or a cut 3-8 f after the press).
- **Real or rebuilt UI** (tiers T1 real capture, T2 rebuilt in vector from the real design). Never generated UI (Part 11 defaults #1: 0 readable glyphs, UI or logos from a video model).
- **Real data**: realistic names, amounts and dates in the target market's locale (₹, Indian grouping 1,00,000 where relevant).
- **Frameless rounded card** by default (radius 24-32 px @1080; ≈30-36 px at 9:16 [inferred, scaled by width]); a device frame only for mobile apps.
- **Shadow:** y ≈24 px, blur ≈48 px, black 6-8%, plus a 1 px hairline at ≈6% (Part 06 defaults #20). Playful styles may use an offset solid backing card (4-6% → 3%).

### 10.2 Legibility contract for phones

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Hero card width | ≈75% W (810 px) in 9:16 |
| Must-read UI text | ≥ 52 px font in 9:16 (floor 36); ≥ 3% H cap in 16:9 (≥ 4% after a cut-in) |
| Anything < 2.5% H | Texture; it may never carry meaning (8/8 references flagged it) |
| Fix for small UI | Enlarge the card, crop into the one row, or cut in 2-3.5× (Chowdeck's 1% H line items are illegible [V:126cpH flaw 3]) |
| UI motion while read | ≤ 0.2% W/f drift, or locked |
| Tilt while read | 0-15°; flatten to 0° in 7-8 f before reading |
| Dim the context | to 50% (secondary) or 15-20% (texture) in 3-6 f, so the one active element reads |
| Simultaneous primary motions | 1 (a cascade counts as one gesture) |

### 10.3 Choreography patterns that fit 15 s

| Pattern (Part 06 §8.11) | Use | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| UI-P6 shared-element morph into a notification | Order/submit → status | Card collapses bottom-anchored over ≈7 f; background rack focus 11-12 f [V:126cpH] |
| UI-P8 click-caused transition | Any proof | Press → consequence 3-8 f |
| UI-P9 before / after toggle | Delta in one shot | Toggle knob 2-3 f; world changes within 3-8 f |
| UI-P1 say → see | AI and voice products | Input card → output 2.6-3.3 s |
| UI-P2 text → UI build | AI features | Border/shadow 1-3 f, icon 3 f, toolbar 4-5 f |

### 10.4 Resolution and raster

UI is vector, or raster at ≥ (max zoom × delivery width) pixels (Part 06 defaults #25). Screen recordings: re-capture at 30 fps on clean demo data, never upscale a 720p capture.

## 11. Sound and music

### 11.1 Music

- **Tempo:** 100 BPM default (beat 18 f, bar 2.4 s); stay inside 83-128 BPM (Part 09 SD-T1). By personality: playful consumer 95-110 (Chowdeck 95.7); energetic launch / kinetic type 100-112; calm AI 85-100. For grid-locked edits prefer whole-frame tempos: 90, 100, 112.5 or 120 BPM (120 also fits the planning grid exactly).
- **One tempo per ad**; raise energy by arrangement (adding high-frequency content), not tempo.
- **Arc for 15 s:** sparse open with a sub accent on f0 → **drop on the first product moment** (1.5-3.0 s, 0-8 f after its cut) → groove → a dip of ≥ 15 dB for 0.3-0.5 s before the decisive tap → groove re-enters → hit on the payoff → sting on the lockup.
- **Arc for 10 s:** intro → groove on the reveal (1.0-1.5 s) → sting at 7.5 s.
- **Arc for 5-6 s:** one bar of groove → sting at 2.4-3.5 s, holding ≈1.2 s and decaying.
- **Ending:** hit or swell on the lockup ±2 f; tail 1.2-1.8 s; the music never stops while the CTA is animating; ≤ 0.3 s of digital silence only at the very end (Part 09 defaults). Lottieicon's music ending 3.7 s before the picture is the anti-pattern [V:1ccYWJ].
- **Re-edit, don't fade:** cut the track on bar lines to land the sting on the lockup; a fade-out mid-phrase reads cheap.
- **Licensing:** TikTok business accounts can use the commercial music library only [N]; regional music where the audience is (Afrobeats/amapiano-adjacent in Chowdeck).

### 11.2 SFX map (Part 09 §16.7)

| Moment | Family | Frame | Level / length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook frame 0 | F12 sub hit or kick | f0 | Peak −2.6 to −9.3 dB; 80-100% of energy below 150 Hz **plus a 100-300 Hz harmonic layer for phone speakers** |
| Hook typing (if typed) | F02 keystrokes | Per burst | 20-25 dB under the full music level |
| Reveal / drop | Music drop, optional F11 hit | On the first product frame, 0-8 f after the cut | +8-15 dB over the bed after a 70-350 ms gap |
| Claim burst | F05 pop / settle tick | First moving frame of the burst | −6 to −10 dB under F01 |
| Whoosh (optional, the ad's only one) | F07 | Peak on the fastest frame of the biggest move | +3 to +6 dB above 4 kHz; 250-700 ms; 0-1 per ad |
| Before the tap | Designed gap / suck-out | 0.3-0.5 s before the press | ≥ 15 dB down, ≤ −35 dB |
| The tap / click | F01 | **Press frame, 0 f** | 20-60 ms, 2-6 kHz; clear inside the dip |
| Toggle | F03 | Press frame; optional second tick 1-3 f later on the settle | 30-80 ms |
| Success / status | F04 soft tone | First full-opacity frame of the result | 150-600 ms; +0-4 dB in its band |
| Counter | F06 ticks ≤ 15/s + a lock accent | Lock on the final-value frame | Low; lock at F01 level |
| Payoff | F11 impact | On the cut ±2 f, after a 70-350 ms gap | +8-15 dB; the ad's one loudest moment |
| Lockup | F17 sting | Within ±2 f of the lockup's first full frame | Sustains 1.2-1.8 s, decays to ≤ −60 dB by the last frame |
| Under everything | F16 ambience / pad | Continuous | Floor −40 to −45 dB; never digital silence mid-ad |

**Density:** ≤ 1 designed sound per beat; sounded transitions 15-65% of transitions, never all; hero hits ≥ 1.4 s apart.

### 11.3 Voice

None under 10 s. At 15 s, VO is optional: ≤ 12 words split across the hook and CTA, on-screen words 0-2 f before their spoken onset, never late; music ducked 10-12 dB under VO (attack 30-80 ms, release 250-700 ms). The ad must still work muted: type carries the whole story (Chowdeck has no VO and is sound-off safe).

### 11.4 Loudness

**−14 LUFS ±1 integrated, ≤ −1 dBTP true peak, LRA 5-10 LU**, measured on the final MP4 (Part 09 §16.11). Audio: AAC-LC 48 kHz stereo 256-320 kb/s; masters as WAV 48 kHz / 24-bit with stems (music, VO, SFX, ambience). NOSTRA's −7.9 LUFS / +1.6 dBTP is clipped; kivi's −10.7 LUFS is turned down ≈3 dB by platforms.

### 12.2 Hook rules

1. **On frame 0, changing by f3** (Part 01 H1). Never fade up from black or open on a logo. A black open is fine only if something hits on f0 (HubSpot's dot + sub impact).
2. **One idea, ≤ 5 words** (H2). Questions are the most common hook (4/8 references), then promises (2/8) and icon/announcement hooks (2/8).
3. **Show the mechanic** (H4): typing for a typing product, a notification for "new", a receipt for an expense app.
4. **In the audience's own idiom** [V:126cpH rule 1]: "You don chow?" in Pidgin for Lagos; Hinglish for Indian reels ("Abhi bhi manually?").
5. **Answer or name by 3 s** (H6).
6. **Size:** cap ≥ 6.5% FH (≥ 70 px cap @1080); hero word 12-13% H in 9:16.

| Hook type | Example (fictional unless cited) | Words | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain question, live-typed | "Still typing?" [V:1-6l8S] / "Still typing receipts?" | 2-3 | Productivity, AI |
| Dialect question | "You don chow?" [V:126cpH] / "Kharcha likha kya?" | 3 | Local consumer apps |
| Time shock (strike) | "~~40 min~~ Now? 3 seconds." | 4-5 | Speed claims |
| Promise slam | "Take payments like a PRO" [V:19NRDv] | 5 | Kinetic launches |
| Objection flip | "Free? Nope." [S:PointCard] | 2 | Performance ads |
| Zero-copy icon | A bell, a receipt, a lock on f1 [V:1ccYWJ] | 0 | "Something new", libraries |
| Event | "For the first time ever" [V:1CSXtQ] | 5 | Integrations, big news |
| Number | "₹0 to file 200 receipts." | 5 | Pricing, outcome |

### 12.3 CTA rules

1. **Verb + destination**, by funnel stage (Part 01 L4). Performance: a button with a verb, ≤ 16 characters, 1-3 words, plus an arrow and a **verified** offer. Awareness: tagline + logo with a soft URL.
2. **Readable:** cap ≥ 5% H; URL ≥ 3.5% H (a 2% H URL was flagged barely legible [V:1ccYWJ]).
3. **One action.** Never "Download, follow and share".
4. **Close the loop** (L3): repeat the hook's word, colour or motif in the lockup (6/8 references).
5. **Platform words:** "Link in bio" (Reels), "Tap Install" (app ads with a platform button), a domain for YouTube and X.

| Funnel | CTA examples |
|---|---|
| Install | "Get the app →" · "Install free" · "Try it free" |
| Trial / sign-up | "Try Snapbill free" · "Start free →" · "Free for 5 users" (sub-line) |
| Offer | "20% off · code FIRST20 · till 31 Oct" (only with a real code and date) |
| Visit / book | "Book a demo" · "See it live →" |
| Awareness / bumper | Tagline + "snapbill.app" |
| Hinglish | "Abhi try karo →" · "Free mein shuru karo" |

### 12.4 Banned or risky copy

Unverifiable superlatives ("#1", "best"), invented numbers or ratings, fake urgency without a date, more than one inline mark (accent, highlight, strike) per scene, and any claim the proof shot does not show.

## 14. Building it with motion-kit vs generative video vs 3D

### 14.1 What motion-kit builds directly

Scene types: hook, kinetic, orb, title, stat, list, compare, bars, grid, quote, prompt, chat, wave, image, clip, cta, logo. Themes: midnight, clean, neon, editorial, pop, desi, corporate, mono, studio, studio-dark. Formats: reel (9:16), portrait (4:5), square (1:1), landscape (16:9). Field reference: `skills/motion-director/references/scenes.md`; recipes in `motion-kit/specs/recipes/` (start from `product-launch-reel.json` for 15 s, `before-after-transformation.json` for a before/after variant, `local-offer-festive.json` or `creator-explainer-hinglish.json` for Indian-market reels).

| Storyboard stage | motion-kit scene | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (time shock / strike) | `hook` | `setup` ≤ 4 words, `strike` ≤ 14 chars, `punch` ≤ 6 words with one `*accent*` |
| Hook (question / slam) | `kinetic` (1-2 lines ≤ 28 chars) or `title` with only a `headline` | Keep the first scene's words on f0 (the checker warns if frame 0 is blank) |
| Setup / pain | `image` (`fit: "cover"`, `move: "in"`, ≤ 8-word caption) or `clip` (`area`, `scrim` 0.5-0.7) | Real photo or a generated plate (`generated: true`) |
| Reveal | `title` (`kicker` ≤ 26 chars, `headline` ≤ 8 words) with `bg: "accent"` for the coral chapter | Max 1-2 `accent`/`inverse` scenes per video |
| Proof: one action | `prompt` (`label`, `prompt` ≤ 40 chars, `button`, `result`) or `chat` | Keep `prompt` short: the kit types ≈2 chars/frame |
| Proof: number | `stat` (`value` ≤ 12 chars counts up; `kicker` = the source) | Only a real number |
| Proof: before/after | `compare` (1-2 rows per side) or `hook` with `strike` | — |
| Payoff | `kinetic` (1-2 lines) or `title` | — |
| CTA | `cta` with `style: "button"` (performance) or `"link"` (awareness) | `action` ≤ 16 chars reads best |
| Lockup | `logo` (`name`, `tagline`, `src`) | Last scene gets the engine's extra 24 f hold |

**Video settings for short ads.** `format: "reel"` master, re-run as `portrait`, `square`, `landscape` (the engine re-lays out; durations stay). `pace: "fast"` (3.6 words/s, 0.4 s hold). `motion: "smooth"` or `"snappy"`; **never `"bouncy"`** on UI (≈3% overshoot on text, ≈10% on buttons). `transition: "cut"` (most cuts in this format are hard cuts). Theme by product: `pop` (consumer apps), `neon` (dev tools), `clean` (productivity), `studio` / `studio-dark` (AI), `desi` (Indian-market reels), `corporate` (B2B). Pipeline: `npm run brand -- <logo> --spec specs/x.json` → `npm run music -- specs/x.json --track music/x.mp3` (snaps cuts to the beat) → `npm run check` → `npm run preview` → `npm run qa` → `npm run make`.

| Gap | Guideline target | Kit behaviour today | Fix |

### 14.3 What needs generative video (Flow / Veo / Sora / Runway / Kling / Higgsfield)

Rule (Part 11 AA-U1): **generate atmosphere, compose meaning.** In a 15 s ad almost everything readable is product truth, so generated footage is limited to one or two plates.

| Layer | Generate? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The "setup" plate (messy desk, receipts, a café counter, a phone buzzing) | Yes | Veo 3.1 / Flow: 4, 6 or 8 s clips at 24 fps; others similar. Generate 1.5-2 s longer than the edit length and cut from the middle: a 4 s clip gives ≤ 2.5 s of edit. One named camera move per clip, or locked off |
| Product or lifestyle B-roll behind the reveal | Optional | Defocus 40-60 px in compositing; `clip` with `generated: true` and `scrim` 0.5-0.7 |
| Abstract texture / light behind type | Yes, or in-engine (`orb`, theme gradients) | 1-2% grain against banding |
| UI, numbers, prices, the logo, the CTA, any readable text | **Never** | 0 readable glyphs from a video model (Part 11 defaults #1) |
| Real people's faces, testimonials | **Never** generated | Real footage with releases, or a `quote` card |
| Generated audio | Discard | Rebuild from the SFX map (§11.2) |

Plate prompt pattern (setup shot): "Top-down locked-off shot of a wooden café table with a messy pile of crumpled paper receipts and a smartphone at the right edge, warm daylight from the top-left, soft shadows, shallow depth of field, a receipt slowly curls, large empty area in the top third. 4 seconds." Negative: "text, letters, numbers, prices, logos, user interface, screen content, watermark, faces, hands". Continuity block repeated verbatim in every prompt (palette hex, key-light direction, lens feel, grade); 2-4 takes; same model and seed per look; conform 24 → 30 fps with optical flow, never frame duplication; log model, prompt, seed and date. Higgsfield's Ads/Marketing Studio can produce the plate set; keep its outputs to plates and composite all type and UI in motion-kit or Remotion.

### 14.4 What needs 3D

At most one beat of 1.4-2.5 s in a 15 s ad; none in 5-10 s ads.

- **Product hero turn** (hardware, packaging): a stepped orbit of 9-12 f per step, never a continuous 360°, one key light matching the 2D key (top-left in the example).
- **Tilted UI plane** for the payoff: the result card tilted ≤ 35° in transit, flattened to 0° in 7-8 f before reading.
- **Flat icon → real object** (HR-06): a 2D icon swapped for a rendered or photographed object on one frame, then a gravity fall [V:126cpH].

Build with `@remotion/three` (UI textures vector or ≥ max zoom × width), After Effects 3D layers, Blender or a 3D scene builder. Roll 0°, no intersecting planes, DOF only on what is not read, motion blur ≈180° on 3D moves.

## 15. QC checklist (Short 5-15 s Motion Ad)

Run on the storyboard, then frame by frame on the locked cut, then on every delivered file.

**Structure and timing**
- [ ] The runtime is exactly the slot length (5.0 / 6.0 / 10.0 / 15.0 s) at 30 fps native; 0 duplicated frames in any move.
- [ ] Stage windows match PL-05 / PL-10 / PL-15 within ±1 beat; the hook is 1.2-2.4 s; the name or answer lands by 3.0 s.
- [ ] Shot count and ASL match the plan within ±10% (15 s: 6-9 shots, ASL 1.5-2.5 s); no shot was shortened just because the ad is short.
- [ ] Every shot has a token; every read/follow shot passes its §17.2 formula with the real copy.
- [ ] Only recognise shots (montage inserts, bridges, punch cards) are under 0.9 s; no text event under 25 f except punch cards in a run of ≥ 3.
- [ ] One transaction (15 s), one action (10 s) or one claim (5-6 s). Nothing else.

**Hook and thumbnail**
- [ ] Frame 0 shows the hook text (or a moving hook object with a hit on f0); the first change happens by f3; nothing is static for > 0.5 s.
