# Specialised Guideline 04: Premium Brand Motion Film

A format guide for the film that sells **what a brand stands for** rather than what a feature does: a brand launch or rebrand, a platform's "it's here" moment, a category or vision film, a keynote opener, a homepage hero. It applies the Master SaaS Motion Design System (Parts 01-11, `research/master/`) to this one format and turns its rules into a plan you can produce. Every number traces to a master section or a reference teardown (`research/videos/`).

**Evidence warning (read first).** None of the eight references is a full cinematic brand film. The master's style card for this format, **ST-B Cinematic Product Launch**, is *assembled* from measured components (kivi's logo materialise, HubSpot's single depth beat and sting, Wix's brand-frame bookends, Lottieicon's light build, Bumper's still lockup) plus text-only sources (P10 §11.2 ST-B, P08 §15.2 last row). Numbers measured in a reference carry a `[V:…]` tag. Numbers that come only from the assembled card carry `[inferred]`. Validate inferred numbers on the first real film before you make them house defaults.

**Reference key** (same tags as the master):

## 1. Purpose and audience

**What the film does.** It makes one promise feel inevitable. It builds a world, introduces a tension, names the brand early but quietly, spends the film's best craft on **one reveal**, shows just enough real product to make the promise credible, and ends on a still lockup that the music resolves on. It sells a *moment* ("it's here", "this is who we are"), not a feature list (P10 ST-B definition).

**How it differs from Guideline 01 (SaaS Product Launch Film):**

| | SaaS launch film (G01) | Premium brand motion film (this guide) |
|---|---|---|
| Job | Show the product doing a job | Make the brand's promise feel true and memorable |
| Product on screen | Proof is a median 58% of runtime (P01 §2.1) | 30-50% of runtime, in ≤3 short proof vignettes |
| The big reveal | Name by about 2.2 s, UI by ≤8 s | Name **seeded** by ≤8 s; the **hero reveal** lands at 44-55% of runtime (P10 ST-B rhythm row [inferred]; HubSpot's drop at 43% [V:1CSXtQ]) |
| ASL | 1.58-3.00 s (references) | 2.5-3.5 s; hero shots 4-8 s (P08 §15.2 [inferred]) |
| Depth | 1 hero beat, 10-15% of runtime | 1 hero reveal at D3-D4 plus ≤2 small depth accents, 10-20% of runtime in total (see §8) |
| Music | 100-127 BPM pluck/pop-electronic | 70-100 BPM, warm-digital hybrid; one long build to one reveal (P09 §16.2.2) |
| End still | ≥1.5 s (2.2 s premium) | **≥2.5 s** (P10 ST-B [inferred]); ≥4 s with a QR |

**Who watches, and where:**

| Audience | Context | What they need | Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prospects, buyers, candidates | Homepage hero (muted autoplay), YouTube, LinkedIn | A feeling of quality and one clear promise | The film must work muted: type carries the story (P01 CD-U6, 6/8 references have no narrator) |
| Press, investors, partners | Launch post, funding announcement, deck embed | Category, ambition, polish | One unforgettable image (the hero reveal) and a lockup they can screenshot |
| Event audience | Keynote opener, conference screen | A shared "here we go" moment | 4K master, longer holds, sound designed for a room |
| Existing customers | Rebrand rollout, email | "Same product, clearer brand" | Show the real product in the new identity at least once |

**Funnel stage:** awareness and brand preference. The CTA is soft ("Try Kestrel →", the URL, "Watch the keynote"), never an offer (P01 §2.11 L4).

## 2. When to choose this format (and when not)

Choose a **Premium Brand Motion Film** when all of these are true:

1. There is **one promise** that fits in ≤12 words, and a tagline that fits in ≤5 (P01 CD-U1, C3).
2. There is a **visual identity to stage**: a logo with an official SVG, one accent hue, a typeface, and ideally a symbol that can act as the film's motif. Without a motif, the film becomes a montage (P01 CD-K1, 8/8 references have a motif with ≥3 roles).
3. There is **at least one real product state** worth showing. A brand film still needs credibility: 6/6 product references show the product or its mechanic by 8 s (P01 R1).
4. The film is an anchor asset (homepage, keynote, launch post) that will be seen more than once, so restraint and long holds pay off.

Pick something else when:

| Situation | Better format | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The goal is feature adoption or a trial sign-up | SaaS launch film (G01) or UI demo (G02) | Proof must take 50-65% of runtime (P01 §2.14 #4); a brand film gives it ≤50% |
| Paid performance placement, 6-15 s | Single-transaction ad (P01 T3) or bumper | ST-B "struggles" below 30 s (P10 §11.6); a 15 s cut keeps only hook, reveal and lockup |
| No visual identity yet (no logo SVG, no accent, no type) | Wait, or make a type-led teaser | A brand film improvises nothing about the brand; generated wordmarks drift (P07 TY-W3, P11 AA-G9) |
| The product has no UI and no visible output | Editorial explainer (P10 ST-F1) or a 3D technology film (ST-D) | Brand films without proof read as "all mood, no product" (§16 #1) |
| The story is a customer outcome | Testimonial spine (P01 T11) | Real voices beat brand voice for consideration |
| You need to explain a mechanism | Energy-chain explainer (P01 T6) | The mechanism needs 21% of runtime and labels |

**Template choice inside this format** (built from P01 §2.4 and P10 §11.1):

| Brand situation | Template | Signature | Nearest evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| New platform, v2, "it's here" | **B1 Tension → turn → reveal** (the default; §4) | Noise collapses into the motif; the hero reveal lands at ≈45% | ST-B [inferred]; reveal mechanics [V:1-6l8S], [V:1CSXtQ] |
| Rebrand / identity launch | **B2 Motif journey** | The new symbol travels through 4-5 worlds, then becomes the logo | Wix heart, 4 roles [V:15VhHR]; NOSTRA circle chain [V:1i2L14] |
| Vision / category film, keynote opener | **B3 Bookended manifesto** | Promise line → 3 worlds → wide calm climax → echo line → lockup | Wix bookends [V:15VhHR]; Solar wide calm climax [V:1Hcg3X t=30.63s] |
| Calm AI or voice brand | **B4 Calm editorial orb** | Near-still orb or waveform as the brand's face; the product's own voice is the hero | ST-A3 [E]; kivi in-world voice [V:1-6l8S] |

## 3. Platforms, aspect ratios and length

### 3.1 Deliverables matrix

| Placement | Aspect | Frame | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage hero, YouTube, X, launch post | 16:9 | 1920×1080 (3840×2160 for flagships) | **60 s master** (45-90 s) | Muted autoplay on the web: the story must read with sound off (P11 §24.7) |
| Keynote / event screen | 16:9 | 3840×2160 | 60-90 s | Deliver music and SFX stems too; the venue mixes the room |
| Website background loop | 16:9 | 1920×1080 or 1280×720 | 10-20 s loop | No audio track; ≈3-5 MB at CRF 20-23; loop point matched in position, colour and motion direction (P11 §24.9 [inferred]) |
| LinkedIn / X feed | 16:9, 4:5 | 1920×1080, 1080×1350 | 30 s cut | Re-layout, not a crop; 4:5 text inside x 88-992 [N] |
| Reels, Shorts, TikTok | 9:16 | 1080×1920 | 15 s (≤30 s) | Text-safe x 120-840, y 270-1210 [N] (P02 §0.2); vertical moves preferred (P04 §6.5) |
| Pre-roll / bumper / sting | 16:9 | 1920×1080 | 6 s | Motif → logo → still ≥1.5 s (P10 §11.6) |

### 3.2 Technical delivery (P11 §24, P09 §16.11)

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Frame rate | **Decide before generating anything.** If generated plates and 3D renders make up more than half the runtime, finish at **24 fps** (Veo 3.1 clips are 24 fps [P]); otherwise 30 fps native. Never pad 24 → 30 by duplicating frames (judder in [V:15VhHR], [V:1CSXtQ], [V:1Hcg3X]) (P11 AA-G11) |
| Codec | H.264, yuv420p, BT.709 tagged, **CRF 16-18**, **12-20 Mb/s** at 1080p; ProRes 422 HQ mezzanine for keynote playback |
| Dark gradients | 1-2% dither on every gradient before the 8-bit encode; banding appears in 4/8 references (P02 CL-U16). Dark canvases band first |
| Audio | **−14 LUFS ±1**, **≤ −1 dBTP**, LRA 5-10 LU; AAC-LC 48 kHz 256-320 kb/s; WAV 48 kHz / 24-bit + stems (drums, bass, music, FX) |
| Captions | Not needed for a type-led film with no VO; burn in any VO line for social |
| Thumbnail | Frame 0 is the feed thumbnail: hook text on f0 (P01 H1). For YouTube, also export a custom thumbnail from the hero-reveal frame |

### 3.3 Length rules

- **Default master: 60 s at 16:9.** 45 s if there are only 2 proof vignettes; 90 s only for a keynote opener or a manifesto with 3 worlds (P10 §11.6: ST-B works at 30, 45, 60 and 90 s).
- Flagship "cinematic launch" films of 2.5-4 min exist (Eleven v3 234 s, Eleven Music 254 s) but are tone references only [E] (P10 ST-B).
- **Cut down by removing whole blocks, never by trimming every shot** (P08 §17.5 #1). The ASL is the film's temperament.
- Below 30 s, keep only hook → reveal → one proof → tagline → lockup (P10 §11.6, "15 s: ST-B struggles").

## 4. Story structure with exact beat timings

### 4.1 The B1 stage model (default template)

| Stage | Job | Share of runtime | Must contain | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | Pose the tension in ≤7 words | 3-7% (1.2-2.4 s) | Text on f0, first change by f3 | P01 H1-H3 (7/8) |
| 2. World / tension | Show the "before" as a visual state, not a sentence | 5-9% | One dense or desaturated world | P01 S2 [V:1-6l8S t=18.57s] |

| 5. Build | Load the reveal: a light build or accelerating move, a riser, a gap | 4-6% | Riser crest 0-2 f before the cut; 70-350 ms gap | P09 F09, SD-SI1 |
| 6. **Hero reveal** | The one image the film is remembered for | 9-18% | At 44-55% of runtime; drop or hit 0-3 f after the picture | P10 ST-B [inferred]; P09 SD-AR1-2 |

## 6. Style direction

### 6.1 Visual concept

- **Concept sentence** (P01 §1.1 #1): "[Tension] becomes [promise], shown as [the motif's journey]". Kestrel example: "The noise of money collapses into one quiet point of light, shown as a dot that becomes a caret, a horizon, a full stop and the logo."
- **Positioning dial** (P01 §1.3): **55 premium / 5 playful / 15 technical / 25 cinematic**. Cinematic stays ≤25%, the ceiling in 8/8 references (CD-P1). Premium comes from restraint, not effects (CD-P2).
- **One motif with 3-5 roles** (P01 CD-K1): Wix's heart has four (emoji → image slot → chip → logo field) [V:15VhHR t=1.03s, 49.63s, 51.37s]. Write the roles down before the storyboard.
- **Two worlds, one rule** (P01 CD-C3): the tension world is desaturated or dense; the brand world is calm, with one accent. Never invert the mapping without a story reason.
- **Light is the material.** Transitions between materials go through light (bloom, wash, light build) or polarity, never through plug-in wipes (P02 CL-U18; kivi routes 12 of 32 transitions through white [V:1-6l8S]).
- **Composition** (P02 §0.2): statement frames ≥85% empty; statement line centred, centre-line y 47-52% H, width 35-70% W; hero UI card 55-70% W; hero object 30-45% W; solo logo 17-26% W; lockup ≤50-57% W.

### 6.2 Colour

**Palette architecture (P02 §11.2, CL-U6):** about 90-95% canvas, 3-8% secondary, ≤5% accent. One accent hue with one meaning (8/8, P01 CD-C2). ≤5 named colours per film, ≤4 per frame (P02 §0.2).

**Default "Ink & Light" palette (dark, ST-B)** — contrast computed (WCAG 2.x):

| Role | Hex | Use | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canvas | #0A0E1A (hue-tinted near-black, dithered) | 90-95% of brand frames | — |
| Surface | #141A2B | Glass card fill at 70-80% opacity | — |
| Ink | #F4F1EA (warm ivory, never #FFFFFF) | All statement type | 17.07 : 1 on canvas |
| Muted | #9AA0AE | Kickers, URL secondary | 7.35 : 1 |
| Accent | #F2B266 (amber) | The motif, the one benefit word per line, the horizon | 10.39 : 1 on canvas; 9.35 : 1 on surface |
| Tension world | #2A2D33 grey, saturation ≤15% | The "before" world | Ivory on it 12.23 : 1 |
| UI surface (inside cards) | #F7F5F0 with ink #1A1A1A | Real product UI | 15.97 : 1; amber text needs #9A5B12 (4.97 : 1) |

**Light "Gallery" variant (calm, ST-A1/A3):** canvas #F7F5F0 or #F9FAFC, ink #1A1A1A, accent text tone ≥4.5 : 1 (for example #9A5B12 or kivi's sage #556962, 5.61 : 1), accent field tones for glow only (P02 §11.13 Airy Aurora preset). Choose light when the product UI is light and the promise is calm or "assistant" (P02 §11.5).

**Rules:**
- **Glow only on emitters** (the motif, the horizon, a screen): halo ≈1-2% of the frame; on type ≤2-3% of cap height (P02 CL-U17; Bumper's 6-8% smeared letters [V:19NRDv t=7.2s]).
- **One flash moment per film at most**, ≤3 f per colour, ≤3 flashes per second [N, WCAG 2.3.1] (P02 CL-U18).
- **One key-light direction for everything**: 2D shadows, 3D lights, glass rims and every generated plate. Default upper-right (P02 CL-U15, CL-U19); Solar's two light directions are the flagged failure [V:1Hcg3X].
- **Grain:** none by default; dither 1-2% on every gradient (P02 §11.12). Grain only in an editorial variant (kit `editorial` theme).
- **No #000 canvas without dither** (Lottieicon bands [V:1ccYWJ]); no pure #FFFFFF canvas in the light variant (NOSTRA never uses it [V:1i2L14]).

### 6.3 Typography (P07)

| Token | Use in this format | Size @1080 (16:9) | Size @1920 (9:16) | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-STATEMENT | Thesis, captions, manifesto lines (3-6 words) | Cap 5.8% H = **90 px font** (68-101) | 128 px | **300-400** (calm premium sets statements at 400; [E] uses 300) |
| T-DISPLAY | Tagline (2-4 words) | Cap 8-11% H = 122-170 px font | 140-200 px | 300-500 |
| T-HERO | One hero word if the film needs it | Cap 12% H = 185 px font | 240 px | 400-500 |
| T-SUPER | Kickers, "Meet", connectors | Cap 4.5% H = 70 px font | 72-106 px | 400-500 |
| T-UI-READ | UI text that must be read | Cap 2.8-4.2% H (43-64 px font) | ≥52 px | UI font |
| T-CTA / T-URL | "Try X →", URL | CTA cap ≥5% H (≥54 px cap ≈ 77 px font); URL ≥3.5% H | 56-64 px | 400-500 |
| T-WORDMARK | Logo | Official SVG only; solo 17-26% W; cap 7-12% H | Cap ≈4% H at 56-62% W | Brand |

- **One sans family** for all copy (8/8), plus at most one accent face with exactly one role, usually the wordmark (P07 TY-W1). Free stand-ins for calm premium: Inter / Inter Tight 300-400, Geist 300 (P07 TY-W2).
- **Tracking** −2 to −3.5% on light display sizes (kit `studio` uses −0.035 em); 0 on lines; 0 to +3% on display caps (P07 #8).
- **Sentence case**, terminal full stops allowed, no exclamation marks (P01 CD-T3, CD-T5).
- **≤6 words per read frame**; typical 2-4 (P07 #11). Two-tone: subject in ink, the benefit phrase in the accent (P01 CD-T4).

## 10. UI treatment

A brand film shows less UI than a launch film, so each UI moment must look finished and real.

1. **Real states only.** Rebuild the real UI as vector layers from the design file or capture at ≥1.5× output width; never generate UI (P06 UI-R; P11 AA-G9). Data looks believable and is approved (P01 CD-U7).
2. **≤3 proof vignettes per 60 s**, each 2.5-6 s, each with one state change and a result held ≥1.0 s (P08 SD-09, SD-17).
3. **Frameless glass cards**, 55-70% W, radius ≈2-3% W, 1 px rim lit from the film's key direction, over a defocused plate (blur ≥3% W) (P02 §0.2; [V:1-6l8S §2]). Device frames only if the device is the product (P01 §1.12 #3).
4. **UI lives in the brand's light.** Its shadow direction, rim and glow obey the one key light (P02 CL-U19). On dark canvases keep UI surfaces just below white (#F7F5F0, not #FFFFFF): a pure-white card on black "reads like a light source" [V:1ccYWJ] (P02 §11.5).
5. **Legibility:** anything that carries meaning has cap ≥3% H at 16:9 (≥52 px at 9:16); everything smaller is texture. Cut in 2-3.5× on the one value rather than shrinking it (P07 floors; P04 CM-U14).
6. **Micro-interactions at real speeds:** toggle 1-3 f, label swap 1 f, dropdown 6 f, row stagger 2-4 f, cursor travel 15-20 f with a 10-12 f ease-out tail, hover 0.3-1.0 s before a click, 1 f state change on the press (P06; [V:15VhHR rule 10]; [V:1CSXtQ rule 7]).
7. **Typing:** filler 28-35 characters/s, the key phrase ≈9 characters/s, 0.9-1.1 s phrase pauses, caret 9 f on / 9 f off, locked at ≈65% W (P07 #18-19; [V:1CSXtQ rule 1]).
8. **One decisive action per film** gets the suck-out treatment (§11.3) (P09 SD-SI2).
9. **3D UI planes:** 4K textures, no greeked or placeholder text, no plane intersections [V:1CSXtQ t=18.4-19.0s].

## 14. Building it: motion-kit versus custom, generative video and 3D

**Production principle (P11 AA-U1):** generate only what is allowed to vary (atmosphere, plates, light); compose everything that must not (UI, type, numbers, logo, motif, product geometry).

### 14.1 What the motion-kit template engine covers

The kit (`motion-kit/`, Remotion 4, 30 fps) builds a film from a JSON spec. Scene types: `hook, kinetic, orb, title, stat, list, compare, bars, grid, quote, prompt, chat, wave, image, clip, cta, logo`. Themes: `midnight, clean, neon, editorial, pop, desi, corporate, mono, studio, studio-dark`. Formats: `reel` 1080×1920, `portrait` 1080×1350, `square` 1080×1080, `landscape` 1920×1080.

**Theme choice for brand films:**

| Brand register | Theme | Theme facts (from `src/engine/themes.ts`) | `motion` | `transition` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dark cinematic ("Ink & Light") | **`studio-dark`** | Warm black #0C0A09, ivory type, display Inter 300, tracking −0.035 em, radius 28, grain on | `calm` (15 f enter, 0% overshoot, blur word-in) | `blur` (default) or `cut` |
| Light calm / AI | `studio` | #F5F5F4 canvas, Inter 300, pastel orb | `calm` | `blur` |
| Luxury, editorial, heritage | `editorial` | Cream #F2ECE2, Instrument Serif 400 display, terracotta accent, grain | `smooth` (22 f, 0% overshoot) | `fade` |
| Swiss / design-led | `mono` | #0C0C0C, Inter 900 caps, one signal red | `smooth` (override the theme's `snappy`) | `cut` |
| Corporate trust | `corporate` | — | `smooth` | `push` / `cut` |

Avoid `midnight`, `neon`, `pop`, `desi` (energetic registers) and **never set `motion: "bouncy"`** (spring overshoot) for this format.

**Kit caveats for this format (checked in the source):**
1. **Accent pops overshoot.** `pop()` in `src/engine/motion.ts` uses a back-out bezier (≈10% overshoot) even under `motion: "calm"`. It drives the `cta` **button** style, the `stat` value, `grid` tiles, `list` styles other than `lines`, the `hook` strike and `chat`/`compare`/`quote` accents. For a premium brand film use `cta` with `style: "link"`, `list` with `style: "lines"`, and avoid `stat`, `grid` and `hook` (the master requires 0% overshoot on type, UI and logos; P03 §19.6).
2. **Reading pace vs. progressive builds.** The kit times text from words per second (relaxed 2.4 wps + 0.9 s hold), so short hooks run 3.0 s instead of 1.2-2.4 s. The master's "references win" rule for progressively built lines (P01 §2.17 #6) is not modelled.
3. **No frame-exact sound events.** SFX are placed per scene (the `logo` scene adds an impact 4 f after the name; `sfx: false` removes it). Gaps, the suck-out and the sting placement must be edited into the music track itself.
4. **Missing devices:** many → one collapse, light build, tonal materialise of an SVG wordmark, punch into a cut, anchored-element montage, 3D. Render them as clips and insert them with `clip`.
5. **Transitions overlap** (≈10 f under `calm`), so the film is shorter than the sum of the scene durations (P11 AA-T9). Add `duration` to proof scenes to reach 60 s rather than adding words.

### 14.2 Custom Remotion or After Effects (deterministic: all type, UI, motif and logo)

Build the noise wall, the dot relay, the tonal wordmark, the tagline punch and the climax montage in Remotion (or AE) with: every value driven by the frame number; clamped `interpolate`; ease tokens from §7.1 only (`Easing.bezier(0.33, 1, 0.68, 1)` etc.); `spring` only as E-CRIT; scale interpolated in log space; fonts loaded with explicit weights before measuring; one string table; the cue sheet generated from the event list; ProRes 4444 (`yuva444p10le`) for transparent overlays (P11 AA-T1-T13). Export each as a `clip` for the kit or assemble the whole film in a custom composition.

### 14.3 What needs generative video (Flow / Veo / Sora / Runway / Kling / Higgsfield)

| Brand-film need | Generate? | How |
|---|---|---|
| Lifestyle "world" plates behind UI (café office, studio, harbour) | **Yes** | One slow move ≤0.2% W/f; nobody in focus; defocus in compositing; one key direction and one grade for every plate (P11 AA-G8, AA-G13) |

| Logo, wordmark, symbol, motif, any text | **No** | Official SVG; composite (P07 TY-W3) |

| Generated audio | **No** | Mute it; score from the cue sheet (AA-G10) |

**Operating rules (P11 AA-G1-G14):** Veo 3.1 clips are 4, 6 or 8 s at 24 fps [P]; generate 1.5-2 s longer than the edit length and use the middle; one named camera move per clip with start and end framing; prompt order Cinematography → Subject → Action → Context → Style, with the continuity block (palette hex, key-light direction, lens, grade, grain) repeated verbatim; ≤3 reference images or ingredients and a fixed seed per look; 2-4 takes per shot, keep the one that passes the anti-AI checklist (P11 §21.4); upscale before compositing; grade all plates together; keep a generation log. The same rules apply in Sora, Runway, Kling and Higgsfield: start from a graded reference still for every plate so style and light stay locked (image-to-video), and never accept a take that drifts in identity or light.

## 15. QC checklist for this format

Run per shot (Gate 1), on the locked cut (Gate 2) and on the final file (Gate 3) (P11 §22). **S1** blocks release; **S2** must be fixed or signed off.

**Story and copy**
- [ ] Hook text on f0, first change by f3; hook 1.2-2.4 s and ≤7 words (S1).
- [ ] Brand name on screen by ≤8 s (S1). The product or its real output by ≤16 s in a 60 s film (S2).
- [ ] Hero reveal at 44-55% of runtime, prepared by a 70-350 ms gap, with the hit or drop 0-3 f after the picture (S2).
- [ ] One concept sentence, one world rule, one accent meaning, one motif with ≥3 roles written in the brief (S2).
- [ ] 20-45 words of film copy per 60 s; ≤6 words per card; no exclamation marks (S2).
- [ ] Tagline ≤5 words and rhymes with the hook (S2).
- [ ] Every claim, number and UI value supplied and approved by the brand; no placeholder text in the master (S1).

**Timing and rhythm**
- [ ] ASL 2.5-3.5 s; hero shot 4-8 s; nothing readable under its hold floor (S2).
- [ ] ≥4 hero moments in 60 s, ≥9 s apart (except the climax montage), each loaded and followed by ≥30 f of calm (S2).
- [ ] One breather of 10-15% of runtime with motion <10% of peaks, under a breakdown (P08 ER-U11) (S2).
- [ ] Climax is the densest edit; any shot under 0.8 s has an anchored element (S2).
- [ ] Final lockup dead still ≥2.5 s (≥4 s with a QR) (S1).
- [ ] Cuts in music-led sections land 0-2 f before the beat, never after (S2).

**Look and motion**
- [ ] One accent hue at ≤5% of the frame; ≤5 named colours; canvas never pure #000 undithered or pure #FFFFFF (S2).
- [ ] One key-light direction across 2D shadows, 3D lights, glass rims and every plate (S2).
- [ ] Glow only on emitters; type glow ≤2-3% of cap height (S2).
- [ ] 0% overshoot on type, UI, logo and camera; no roll; no spinning objects (S2).
- [ ] DOF and parallax on ≤3 beats; one D4 beat at 8-15% of runtime (S2).
- [ ] At most 1 flash moment, ≤3 f per colour, ≤3 flashes per second (S1, photosensitivity).
- [ ] No 1-frame artefacts at cuts; no 1-frame dip to black: step every cut ±10 f frame by frame (S1).

**UI, type and brand**
- [ ] UI is real (design file or capture ≥1.5× width), never generated; no greeked UI on 3D planes; no plane intersections (S1).
- [ ] Must-read text cap ≥3% H at 16:9 (≥52 px at 9:16); sentences ≥84 px font for phone-inline 16:9 (S2).
- [ ] Logo, wordmark and motif are the official vectors, identical in every shot and every cutdown (S1).
- [ ] Text-safe: 16:9 x 96-1824, y 54-1026; 9:16 x 120-840, y 270-1210 (S1 for clipped text).
- [ ] Contrast ≥4.5:1 (3:1 only for large text; 7:1 over footage or plates) (S1).

**Generated and 3D layers**
- [ ] No glyphs, signage, screens, faces or hands in any plate; continuity block used; generation log kept (S1).
- [ ] No texture boil, identity drift or lighting change across a plate's used range (P11 AA-06, AA-09, AA-13) (S1).
- [ ] Frame rate decided before generation; no 24 → 30 duplicate-frame padding (S2).

**Sound**
- [ ] −14 LUFS ±1, ≤ −1 dBTP, measured on the final file (S1 for overs).
- [ ] Hit or sting within ±2 f of the lockup's first full frame; music ends with or after the last frame; the CTA never plays over silence (S1).
- [ ] 9-15 designed sounds per 60 s; ≤2 whooshes; ≤2 risers; mid-film floor −40 to −45 dB (S2).
- [ ] The film works muted (S2; homepage heroes autoplay muted).

**Delivery**
- [ ] 1920×1080 or larger (4K for keynote), native fps, H.264 CRF 16-18 at 12-20 Mb/s, BT.709 tagged, dither on gradients (S2).
- [ ] 30 s, 15 s and 6 s versions re-laid out (not cropped), cut by whole blocks, music re-edited on bar lines (S2).

## 16. Common mistakes in brand films (and fixes)

| # | Mistake | Seen in / source | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | All mood, no product: the brand appears only at the end, or the product never appears | NOSTRA names itself at 92% of runtime [V:1i2L14 t=32.17s] | Seed the name by ≤8 s; show one real product state by ≤16 s |
| 2 | "Cinematic" everywhere: DOF, parallax and 3D on every shot | ST-B avoid line (P04 DP-A1); 8/8 references keep cinematic ≤25% | One D4 hero beat at 8-15% of runtime; flat, calm 2D elsewhere |
| 3 | Epic trailer music (drums, choirs, braams) | None of the eight references use it (P09 SD-MU3) | Warm-digital hybrid at 70-100 BPM; build by brightness, not volume |
| 4 | Reveal with no load: the logo lands mid-groove with no change in the mix | P01 R2 [inferred] | 70-350 ms gap + riser crest 0-2 f before the cut; drop 0-3 f after the picture |
| 5 | Generated wordmark, UI or numbers | P11 AA-05, AA-G9; Veo adds gibberish text [P] | Official SVG and real UI as composited layers; negative-prompt text and screens |
| 6 | Plates that do not match (style, light, grade) | kivi's three illustration styles [V:1-6l8S]; Bumper's one stock photo [V:19NRDv t=29.97s]; Solar's two light directions [V:1Hcg3X] | One reference still, one seed, one continuity block, one grade pass |

| 9 | Glow and light leaks as decoration | P02 CL-U17; Bumper's bloom smears letters [V:19NRDv t=7.2s] | Glow only on emitters; ≤2-3% of cap height on type |
